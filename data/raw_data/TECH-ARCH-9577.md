# 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

## 3.1 Compliance Monitoring
The Architecture department will regularly monitor architectural decisions and software development activities to ensure compliance with company standards and policies.

### 3.1.1 Audits and Assessments
The department will conduct regular audits and assessments to evaluate adherence to architectural principles, patterns, and SOPs.

### 3.1.2 Corrective Actions
In case of non-compliance, the Architecture department will work with stakeholders to develop and implement corrective actions to bring systems back into compliance.

## 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

### 3.2.1 Warning and Education
The first instance of non-compliance may result in a warning and education on company standards and policies.

### 3.2.2 Review and Approval Hold
Subsequent instances of non-compliance may result in a review and approval hold on architectural decisions or software development activities until compliance is achieved.

## 3.3 Operational Governance

### 3.3.1 Decision-Making Process
The Architecture department will follow a structured decision-making process, involving the ARB and other stakeholders as necessary, to ensure informed and aligned decision-making.

### 3.3.2 Stakeholder Communication
Regular communication with stakeholders is essential for effective governance and transparency in architectural decisions and software development activities.
