## 1. Document Scope and Executive Summary

This reference document outlines the core policies, standard operating procedures (SOPs), and operational governance framework for the Human Resources (HR) department at [Company Name]. The purpose of this document is to provide a comprehensive guide for HR staff, management, and employees on the processes and practices that govern HR operations. This document also serves as a reference point for compliance monitoring and enforcement.

## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Employee Onboarding and Offboarding

* Procedure: All new hires will undergo a comprehensive onboarding process, which includes:
	+ Completion of necessary paperwork and forms
	+ Introduction to HR staff and departmental contacts
	+ Review of company policies and procedures
	+ Training on company systems and tools
* SOP: The HR team will ensure that all necessary documentation is completed and filed within 3 days of employee start date. A checklist will be used to track progress.

### 2.2 Time-Off Request and Approval

* Policy: Employees are entitled to a minimum of [X] paid time-off days per year, as outlined in the company's annual leave policy.
* Procedure:
	+ Employees must submit time-off requests at least [Y] days in advance
	+ Managers will review and approve/deny requests within [Z] business hours
	+ HR will maintain a centralized calendar for approved time-offs

### 2.3 Performance Management and Evaluation

* Policy: Regular performance evaluations will be conducted to assess employee performance and provide feedback.
* Procedure:
	+ Employees will receive a performance evaluation form at least [X] months prior to their anniversary date
	+ Managers will conduct the evaluation, providing constructive feedback and setting goals for improvement
	+ HR will maintain records of all performance evaluations

### 2.4 Recruitment and Hiring

* Policy: All job openings will be posted internally before being advertised externally.
* Procedure:
	+ Job descriptions will be created and approved by hiring managers
	+ Resumes and applications will be reviewed and screened by HR staff
	+ Candidates will be selected for interviews based on a standardized scoring system

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

* Procedure: The HR department will conduct regular compliance audits to ensure adherence to company policies and procedures.
* Frequency: Audits will be conducted quarterly, with a focus on specific areas of concern (e.g., time-off requests, performance evaluations).

### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

* Policy: Failure to comply with HR policies and procedures may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination.
* Procedure:
	+ Employees found to be non-compliant will receive a written warning or formal disciplinary action
	+ Repeated offenses may lead to more severe penalties

### 3.3 Operational Governance

* Policy: The HR department will maintain accurate and up-to-date records of all employee data, ensuring compliance with relevant laws and regulations.
* Procedure:
	+ Employee records will be reviewed and updated annually
	+ HR staff will ensure that all records are secure and accessible only to authorized personnel
