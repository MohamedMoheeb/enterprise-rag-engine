# TECH-ARCH-4576: Architecture Reference Document
## Department: Architecture
## Author: Alexis Burton
## Effective Date: 2023-02-01

### Document Scope and Executive Summary

This document outlines the architecture reference guidelines for the Architecture department. The purpose of this document is to provide a comprehensive framework for architects, engineers, and other stakeholders involved in designing and implementing technology solutions within the organization.

The scope of this document includes:

* Defining the core policies and standard operating procedures (SOPs) for the Architecture department
* Establishing compliance monitoring and penalties for non-compliance
* Outlining operational governance and management structures

### 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

#### Policy: Architectural Design Principles

The Architecture department shall adhere to the following design principles:

* Follow a modular, service-oriented architecture (SOA) approach
* Utilize open standards and industry-recognized protocols
* Prioritize scalability, reliability, and maintainability
* Ensure secure and auditable data storage and processing

#### SOP: Request for Architecture Support

To request architecture support, submit a request to the Architecture department via [insert ticketing system or email address]. The request should include:

* A clear description of the problem or opportunity
* Relevant technical requirements and constraints
* Expected outcomes and benefits

The Architecture department shall respond within 2 business days with an estimated timeline for completing the requested work.

#### SOP: Change Management Process

The Architecture department shall follow a formal change management process for all architecture-related changes. The process includes:

1. Request for Change (RFC): Submit a RFC to the Architecture department via [insert ticketing system or email address]
2. Review and Approval: The Architecture department shall review and approve the RFC within 3 business days
3. Implementation: Implement the approved change, following established procedures and guidelines

### 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

#### Compliance Monitoring

The Architecture department shall conduct regular compliance monitoring to ensure adherence to established policies and SOPs. This includes:

* Quarterly reviews of architecture designs and implementations
* Annual audits of architecture-related documentation and records

#### Penalties for Non-Compliance

Failure to comply with established policies and SOPs may result in penalties, including but not limited to:

* Suspension or revocation of architecture privileges
* Mandatory training or certification requirements
* Performance-based adjustments or terminations

#### Operational Governance

The Architecture department shall maintain accurate and up-to-date records of all architecture-related activities. This includes:

* Documenting design decisions and rationale
* Maintaining a version control system for architecture documents
* Conducting regular retrospectives to identify lessons learned and areas for improvement
