## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Employee Conduct Policy

* Purpose: To maintain a positive and respectful work environment by promoting ethical behavior and professional conduct among employees.
* Scope: All employees are expected to adhere to the principles outlined in this policy, which applies to all aspects of their employment.

### 2.2 Recruitment and Hiring Process

* SOP:
	+ Job postings and requirements will be reviewed and approved by HR and relevant stakeholders.
	+ Candidate screening and interviews will be conducted according to established criteria.
	+ New hires will undergo a comprehensive onboarding program, including training and orientation.

### 2.3 Employee Performance Management

* Policy: Regular performance evaluations will be conducted to assess employee achievement of goals and objectives.
* SOP:
	+ Performance evaluation forms will be reviewed and approved by HR and relevant stakeholders.
	+ Employees will receive feedback and coaching to improve performance and address areas for development.

### 2.4 Benefits Administration

* Policy: [Company Name] will provide a comprehensive benefits package, including [list specific benefits].
* SOP:
	+ Benefits enrollment and administration will be handled through an online platform or HR system.
	+ Employees will receive regular updates on benefits and changes to the program.

### 2.5 Employee Relations and Conflict Resolution

* Policy: To maintain a positive work environment, [Company Name] encourages open communication and addresses employee concerns in a timely and fair manner.
* SOP:
	+ Employee concerns will be documented and addressed through a formal process, involving HR and relevant stakeholders.
	+ Confidentiality will be maintained throughout the resolution process.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

* Responsibility: HR will monitor compliance with all policies, procedures, and laws governing employment practices.
* Frequency: Regular audits and spot checks will be conducted to ensure adherence to established guidelines.

### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

* Policy: Failure to comply with HR policies or procedures may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment.
* Procedure:
	+ Incidents will be documented and investigated according to established protocols.
	+ Employees found to have engaged in non-compliant behavior will receive appropriate disciplinary action.

### 3.3 Operational Governance

* Policy: [Company Name] is committed to maintaining transparency and accountability in all HR operations.
* SOP:
	+ Records will be kept of all HR activities, including employee data and transactions.
	+ Access to sensitive information will be restricted according to established protocols.
