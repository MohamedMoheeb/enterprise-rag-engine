# Architecture Reference Document: TECH-ARCH-4165

## Department Information

* Department: Architecture
* Author: Raymond Smith
* Effective Date: 2022-05-06

## 1. Document Scope and Executive Summary

This document outlines the core policies, standard operating procedures (SOPs), and governance framework for the Architecture department. The purpose of this reference guide is to provide a comprehensive overview of the department's operations, ensuring consistency and transparency across all projects.

The scope of this document includes:

* Defining the Architecture department's role and responsibilities
* Outlining core policies and SOPs governing project development, design, and delivery
* Establishing compliance monitoring procedures and penalties for non-compliance
* Providing operational governance guidelines for effective project management

## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Project Initiation and Planning

* All projects must be initiated through the department's official project management tool (Asana) with clear objectives, timelines, and resource allocation.
* The Architecture team will collaborate with stakeholders to define project scope, goals, and deliverables.

### 2.2 Design Principles and Guidelines

* The Architecture team will adhere to established design principles and guidelines for all projects, including:
	+ Usability
	+ Accessibility
	+ Scalability
	+ Security
	+ Performance
* All designs must be reviewed and approved by the department's Design Review Committee.

### 2.3 Development and Testing

* The Architecture team will use version control systems (Git) for all project development.
* A comprehensive testing framework will be established to ensure the quality of delivered products.

### 2.4 Change Management and Version Control

* All changes must be tracked and documented using the department's official change management tool (Jira).
* The Architecture team will maintain a version control system for all projects, ensuring consistent updates and releases.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

* Regular audits and reviews will be conducted to ensure adherence to established policies and SOPs.
* The department's Quality Assurance Committee will monitor project progress and quality.

### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

* Failure to comply with departmental policies or SOPs may result in:
	+ Verbal warnings
	+ Written warnings
	+ Suspension of privileges
	+ Disciplinary action, up to and including termination

### 3.3 Operational Governance

* The Architecture team will maintain accurate records of all project activities, including timelines, milestones, and deliverables.
* The department's Project Management Office (PMO) will oversee the coordination and execution of projects.

By adhering to this document, the Architecture department can ensure consistent delivery of high-quality products and services while maintaining compliance with established policies and SOPs.
