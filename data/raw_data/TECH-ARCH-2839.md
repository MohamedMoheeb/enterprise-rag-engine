## Department: Architecture
## Author: Gregory Kelly
## Effective Date: 2020-09-21

### Document Scope and Executive Summary

This document serves as the authoritative reference for the Architecture department, outlining core policies, standard operating procedures (SOPs), and compliance guidelines. The purpose of this document is to provide a unified framework for architecture-related activities, ensuring consistency, efficiency, and adherence to regulatory requirements.

The scope of this document encompasses all architecture-related activities, including design, development, testing, deployment, and maintenance of architectural systems, frameworks, and solutions within the organization.

### 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

#### 2.1 Architecture Design Principles

* Adherence to established architecture principles and patterns
* Alignment with organizational goals and strategic objectives
* Consistent application of design standards and guidelines

#### 2.2 Change Management

* Identification, evaluation, and prioritization of changes
* Implementation of change management processes (CM) for new or modified architectures
* Continuous monitoring and review of architectural components and systems

#### 2.3 Architecture Review Board (ARB)

* Establishment and maintenance of an ARB to oversee architecture-related activities
* Review and approval of proposed architectures, modifications, and changes
* Identification and mitigation of potential risks and conflicts

#### 2.4 Documentation and Knowledge Management

* Maintenance of accurate and up-to-date documentation for architectural components and systems
* Creation and dissemination of knowledge artifacts (e.g., design documents, technical specifications)
* Continuous updating and refinement of documentation and knowledge management processes

### 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

#### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

* Ongoing monitoring of architecture-related activities for compliance with organizational policies, regulations, and industry standards
* Identification and reporting of non-compliant activities or systems

#### 3.2 Penalties and Corrective Actions

* Implementation of penalties and corrective actions for non-compliance, as defined by organizational policies and procedures
* Continuous evaluation and refinement of penalties and corrective actions to ensure their effectiveness

#### 3.3 Operational Governance

* Establishment and maintenance of operational governance processes for architecture-related activities
* Definition of roles and responsibilities for architects, stakeholders, and other involved parties
* Continuous monitoring and review of operational governance processes to ensure their effectiveness
