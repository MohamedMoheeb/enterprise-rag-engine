# TECH-ARCH-9137: Architecture Reference Document
## Department: Architecture
## Author: Amanda Summers
## Effective Date: 2025-12-25


### Document Scope and Executive Summary
This reference document outlines the core policies, standard operating procedures, and operational governance for the Architecture department. The purpose of this document is to provide a comprehensive guide for architects, engineers, and other stakeholders involved in the design and development of our company's products and services.

The scope of this document includes:

* Definition of architectural principles and standards
* Description of the architecture review process
* Guidelines for creating and maintaining architecture documents
* Compliance monitoring and penalty procedures
* Operational governance and decision-making processes

### Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures


#### 1.1. Architecture Principles

Our company's architecture principles are designed to ensure that our products and services are scalable, maintainable, and meet customer needs. The following principles apply:

* Separation of Concerns: Each system component should have a single responsibility.
* Single Responsibility Principle (SRP): Each module or class should have only one reason to change.
* Open/Closed Principle (OCP): Software entities should be open for extension but closed for modification.

#### 1.2. Architecture Review Process

The architecture review process is used to ensure that all architectural decisions are made in accordance with our company's principles and standards. The process involves:

* Submission of architecture documents by architects
* Peer review by other architects or engineers
* Approval or rejection based on compliance with our company's principles and standards

#### 1.3. Architecture Document Creation and Maintenance


##### 1.3.1. Architecture Document Templates

Architecture documents should be created using standardized templates to ensure consistency and ease of use.

##### 1.3.2. Architecture Document Content

Architecture documents should include:

* System overview
* Component descriptions
* Interfaces and interactions
* Non-functional requirements (e.g., performance, security)

### Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance


#### 2.1. Compliance Monitoring

The architecture team will monitor compliance with our company's principles and standards by:

* Reviewing architecture documents for adherence to our company's principles and standards
* Conducting regular audits of system components and interfaces

#### 2.2. Penalties and Consequences

If a system component or interface is found to be non-compliant, the following penalties may apply:

* Architectural changes may require additional time or resources
* System modifications may be necessary to ensure compliance
* Non-compliance may result in project delays or cancellations

#### 2.3. Operational Governance and Decision-Making Processes


##### 2.3.1. Architecture Team Responsibilities

The architecture team is responsible for:

* Developing and maintaining our company's principles and standards
* Reviewing and approving architecture documents
* Providing guidance on architectural decisions

##### 2.3.2. Decision-Making Process

Decisions regarding architecture will be made through a collaborative process involving the architecture team, engineers, and other stakeholders. The decision-making process will consider:

* Business objectives and goals
* Technical feasibility and constraints
* Customer needs and requirements
