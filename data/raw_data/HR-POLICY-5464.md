## 1. Document Scope and Executive Summary
This HR Reference Document outlines the fundamental policies, standard operating procedures (SOPs), and compliance requirements for the Human Resources department at [Company Name]. The scope of this document covers key aspects of HR operations, including employee onboarding, performance management, compensation and benefits, and personnel records management. This comprehensive guide aims to provide a unified framework for HR processes, ensuring consistency and efficiency across all departments.

## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures
### 2.1 Employee Onboarding

* All new hires must undergo a mandatory orientation program within the first week of employment.
* The onboarding process will include:
	+ Completion of required paperwork (I-9, W-4, etc.)
	+ Review of employee handbook and company policies
	+ Introduction to HR representative and supervisor
	+ Access to necessary IT resources and training

### 2.2 Performance Management

* Annual performance evaluations will be conducted for all employees.
* Evaluation criteria will include job knowledge, communication skills, teamwork, and overall job performance.
* Employees will receive written feedback and a rating (1-5) based on their performance.

### 2.3 Compensation and Benefits

* Salary increases will be granted annually based on individual performance and company-wide budget constraints.
* The company offers a comprehensive benefits package, including:
	+ Medical insurance
	+ Dental insurance
	+ Vision insurance
	+ 401(k) retirement plan
	+ Paid time off (PTO)

### 2.4 Personnel Records Management

* All employee records will be maintained electronically through our HR Information System (HRIS).
* Employee files must include:
	+ Job descriptions and performance evaluations
	+ Training and development records
	+ Compensation and benefits information
	+ Attendance and leave records

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance
### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

* The HR department will conduct regular audits to ensure compliance with company policies and federal/state regulations.
* Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

* First-time offenders: Verbal warning
* Second-time offenders: Written warning
* Third-time offenders: Suspension (up to 1 week)
* Repeat offenders: Termination of employment

### 3.3 Operational Governance

* The HR department will maintain accurate and up-to-date records in accordance with relevant laws and regulations.
* All HR staff must adhere to confidentiality agreements and maintain the integrity of employee data.
