## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

The Compliance Department is responsible for monitoring and enforcing compliance with regulatory requirements and internal policies. This includes:

* Conducting regular audits and risk assessments
* Investigating potential compliance issues or violations
* Implementing corrective actions and training programs as needed

### 3.2 Penalties and Disciplinary Actions

Violations of compliance policies and procedures may result in disciplinary actions, including:

* Verbal warnings
* Written warnings
* Suspension or termination of employment

### 3.3 Operational Governance

The Compliance Department operates under the following governance framework:

* **Compliance Committee**: A senior-level committee responsible for overseeing compliance initiatives and ensuring effective risk management.
* **Risk Management Framework**: A structured approach to identifying, assessing, and mitigating operational risks that could impact compliance.

### 3.4 Reporting and Communication

The Compliance Department is responsible for reporting compliance issues and incidents to the appropriate stakeholders, including:

* Senior management
* Board of Directors
* Regulatory bodies
