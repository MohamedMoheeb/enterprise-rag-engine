## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Employee Recruitment and Onboarding
* All job postings must be approved by HR before publication.
* Job applicants will receive an automated response acknowledging receipt of their application.
* New hires will undergo a comprehensive onboarding process, including orientation, training, and familiarization with company policies.

### 2.2 Performance Management
* The performance review cycle will occur bi-annually, with clear goals and objectives set for each employee.
* Employees will receive regular feedback from managers/supervisors to ensure effective goal alignment.

### 2.3 Employee Conduct and Code of Conduct
* All employees are expected to adhere to our Code of Conduct, which outlines expectations around ethics, integrity, and professionalism.
* Any misconduct or violation of the Code will be investigated and addressed according to established procedures.

### 2.4 Time-Off and Leave Policies
* Employees will receive [X] days/year paid time-off (PTO) for vacation, sick leave, and other personal reasons.
* PTO requests must be submitted a minimum of [X] business days in advance.


## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring
* The HR department will maintain records of all employee-related activities, ensuring compliance with relevant laws, regulations, and company policies.
* Regular audits will be conducted to verify adherence to established procedures.

### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance
* Failure to comply with HR policies or SOPs may result in disciplinary actions up to and including termination of employment.

### 3.3 Operational Governance
* The HR department will maintain open communication channels with employees, management, and other stakeholders.
* All HR-related matters must be submitted through the designated HR portal for tracking and resolution.
