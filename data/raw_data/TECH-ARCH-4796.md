## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Design Principles

* Adhere to the company's design standards and guidelines
* Ensure designs are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with business objectives
* Prioritize user experience, accessibility, and usability in all design decisions

### 2.2 Project Management

* Utilize Agile methodologies for project delivery (Scrum, Kanban)
* Establish clear project scope, timelines, and resource allocation
* Conduct regular team meetings and retrospectives to ensure effective communication and continuous improvement

### 2.3 Architecture Review Process

* Develop and maintain a comprehensive architecture review process for all design decisions
* Ensure reviews are conducted by multiple stakeholders, including architects, engineers, and business representatives
* Document and track all architectural decisions, including rationale and impact analysis

### 2.4 Change Management

* Establish a change management process for architectural changes, including assessment, approval, and implementation
* Identify potential risks and mitigation strategies for each proposed change
* Communicate changes to stakeholders and ensure minimal disruption to ongoing projects

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

* Maintain a compliance dashboard to track progress and monitor trends

### 3.2 Penalties and Corrective Actions

* Implement penalties for non-compliance, such as re-work or additional training
* Develop and implement corrective actions for identified compliance issues

* Establish a governance framework for architecture operations, including decision-making processes and escalation procedures
