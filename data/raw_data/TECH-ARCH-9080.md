## 1. Document Scope and Executive Summary

This Architecture Reference Document outlines the scope, policies, and standard operating procedures for the Architecture department. The document aims to provide a comprehensive framework for the design, development, testing, and maintenance of software architectures within the organization.

The primary objectives of this document are:

* To establish a set of core principles and guidelines for architecture-related activities
* To define the roles and responsibilities of architecture stakeholders
* To outline the processes and procedures for architecture development, review, and approval

## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Architecture Development Process

The architecture development process involves the following stages:

1. **Requirements Gathering**: Identify business requirements and define functional and non-functional requirements.
2. **Design**: Develop high-level and low-level design documents, including system architecture diagrams and technical specifications.
3. **Implementation**: Implement the designed architecture, ensuring compliance with technical standards and guidelines.
4. **Testing**: Conduct thorough testing to ensure the architecture meets the required criteria.
5. **Deployment**: Deploy the architecture to production environments, following established change management procedures.

### 2.2 Architecture Review and Approval

The architecture review and approval process involves the following steps:

1. **Architecture Design Review**: Review the design documents for completeness, accuracy, and adherence to technical standards.
2. **Technical Review Committee (TRC) Meeting**: Present the design to the TRC, addressing any questions or concerns raised by committee members.
3. **Final Approval**: Obtain final approval from the Architecture Lead or designated approver.

### 2.3 Architecture Change Management

The architecture change management process involves the following steps:

1. **Request for Change (RFC)**: Submit a formal RFC to propose changes to existing architectures.
2. **Impact Assessment**: Conduct an impact assessment to determine the potential effects of the proposed changes.
3. **Change Implementation**: Implement the approved changes, ensuring minimal disruption to existing systems.

### 2.4 Architecture Documentation and Knowledge Management

The architecture documentation and knowledge management process involves the following steps:

1. **Documentation Creation**: Create and maintain accurate and up-to-date documentation for all architectures.
2. **Knowledge Sharing**: Share knowledge and expertise among team members through training, mentorship, and collaboration.
3. **Knowledge Retention**: Retain knowledge gained from past projects to improve future architecture development.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

The Architecture department will monitor compliance with the policies and procedures outlined in this document through regular audits, inspections, and reviews.

### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

In the event of non-compliance with these policies and procedures, penalties may include:

* Verbal warnings
* Written warnings
* Suspension or termination of architecture-related activities
* Revocation of architecture certification or credentials

### 3.3 Operational Governance

The Architecture department will ensure operational governance through the following mechanisms:

1. **Architecture Lead**: Appoint an Architecture Lead to oversee and coordinate architecture-related activities.
2. **Architecture Committee**: Establish an Architecture Committee to review and approve architectures, as well as provide guidance and support.
3. **Change Management Process**: Implement a change management process to track and manage changes to existing architectures.

By following the guidelines outlined in this document, the Architecture department will ensure the development of high-quality software architectures that meet business requirements and technical standards.
