# TECH-ARCH-1109: Architecture Reference Document

## Department: Architecture
## Author: Christopher Ross
## Effective Date: 2021-02-20

### Executive Summary:

This document outlines the reference guidelines for the Architecture department, encompassing core policies, standard operating procedures (SOPs), and compliance monitoring. The purpose is to establish a framework for effective architecture design, development, and deployment within our organization.

### Document Scope:

This document applies to all employees, contractors, and partners involved in architectural activities across various projects and initiatives within the organization.

## 1. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### Policy 1: Architecture Design Principles

* The Architecture department will adhere to established design principles, prioritizing scalability, maintainability, security, and performance.
* All architecture designs must be reviewed and approved by a designated subject matter expert (SME) before implementation.

### SOP 1: Architecture Request Process

1. Submit requests for architectural services through the internal project management tool.
2. The request will be reviewed and prioritized by the Architecture department based on business requirements and resource availability.
3. Approved requests will be assigned to a dedicated architect for design and development.
4. Regular updates and progress reports will be shared with stakeholders.

### SOP 2: Code Review and Quality Assurance

1. All code changes must undergo peer review by at least one other developer or architect.
2. The Architecture department will conduct regular quality assurance checks to ensure adherence to established coding standards and best practices.

## 2. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### Compliance Monitoring:

The Architecture department will maintain a compliance tracking system to monitor adherence to established policies and procedures. This includes regular audits, monitoring of code changes, and performance metrics analysis.

### Penalties for Non-Compliance:

* First-time violators: Verbal warning and mandatory training on architectural design principles and SOPs.
* Repeat offenders: Written reprimand and temporary suspension from architecture-related activities.

### Operational Governance:

The Architecture department will maintain a governance framework, outlining roles and responsibilities, decision-making processes, and communication channels. This includes regular meetings with stakeholders to ensure alignment and address concerns.

## Final Thoughts

This document serves as a foundational reference for the Architecture department's policies, procedures, and compliance expectations. It is essential that all employees, contractors, and partners understand and adhere to these guidelines to ensure effective architecture design, development, and deployment within our organization.
