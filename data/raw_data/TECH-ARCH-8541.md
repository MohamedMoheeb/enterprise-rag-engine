# TECH-ARCH-8541: Architecture Reference Document
## Department: Architecture
## Author: Jacob Snyder
## Effective Date: 2023-05-29


# 1. Document Scope and Executive Summary
This document serves as a comprehensive reference guide for the Architecture department, outlining core policies, standard operating procedures, and compliance monitoring protocols. The purpose of this document is to ensure consistency and efficiency in architectural design, development, and deployment across all projects.

The scope of this document includes:

* Defining the role and responsibilities of the Architecture team
* Establishing guidelines for architecture design and decision-making
* Outlining standard operating procedures for architectural development and deployment
* Specifying compliance monitoring and penalty frameworks

This document is intended for all employees within the Architecture department, as well as stakeholders and project managers who interact with the Architecture team.


# 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures


### 2.1 Architecture Design Principles
The Architecture team shall adhere to the following design principles:

* Scalability: Designs must be scalable to accommodate growth and changing requirements.
* Security: Designs must prioritize security and confidentiality of data and systems.
* Maintainability: Designs must be maintainable, with clear documentation and easy updates.
* Compatibility: Designs must be compatible with existing infrastructure and systems.

### 2.2 Architecture Development Process
The Architecture team shall follow the following development process:

1. Requirements gathering: Gather requirements from stakeholders and project managers.
2. Design creation: Create high-level and detailed design documents.
3. Review and feedback: Obtain feedback and iterate on designs as necessary.
4. Implementation: Implement designed solutions, ensuring compliance with architecture principles.

### 2.3 Architecture Deployment Process
The Architecture team shall follow the following deployment process:

1. Testing: Conduct thorough testing to ensure solution meets requirements.
2. Deployment planning: Plan deployment strategy and timeline.
3. Deployment execution: Execute deployment, monitoring for any issues.
4. Post-deployment review: Review post-deployment performance and make adjustments as necessary.

### 2.4 Change Management Process
The Architecture team shall follow the following change management process:

1. Request submission: Submit requests for changes to existing architectures or systems.
2. Evaluation: Evaluate requests based on feasibility, impact, and alignment with architecture principles.
3. Approval: Obtain approval from relevant stakeholders and project managers.
4. Implementation: Implement approved changes, ensuring compliance with architecture principles.

### 2.5 Architecture Documentation
The Architecture team shall maintain accurate and up-to-date documentation for all architectures and systems. This includes:

* High-level design documents
* Detailed design documents
* Deployment plans
* Post-deployment reviews

### 2.6 Code Quality and Style Guidelines
The Architecture team shall adhere to the following code quality and style guidelines:

* Follow established coding standards and best practices.
* Use consistent naming conventions and formatting.
* Ensure code is readable, maintainable, and efficient.

### 2.7 Collaboration and Communication
The Architecture team shall collaborate and communicate effectively with stakeholders, project managers, and other teams, including:

* Regular meetings and updates
* Clear and concise communication of design decisions and changes
* Active participation in cross-functional teams and working groups


# 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring
The Architecture team shall monitor compliance with architecture principles, standard operating procedures, and policies on a regular basis.

### 3.2 Penalty Framework
Violations of architecture principles, standard operating procedures, or policies shall be subject to the following penalty framework:

* First offense: Verbal warning and mandatory training.
* Second offense: Written warning and suspension of privileges.
* Third offense: Suspension of employment.

### 3.3 Operational Governance
The Architecture team shall operate under the following governance structure:

* Architecture Lead: Responsible for overall architecture strategy and direction.
* Architecture Team: Responsible for design, development, and deployment of architectures.
* Stakeholders and Project Managers: Provide requirements gathering and feedback.
* Management: Provides oversight, guidance, and resource allocation.

This document is effective as of the Effective Date listed above. Any changes or updates to this document shall be reviewed and approved by the Architecture Lead before implementation.
