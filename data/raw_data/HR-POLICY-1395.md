# HR Reference Document: HR-POLICY-1395
## Department: HR
## Author: John Rosales
## Effective Date: 2021-08-07

### Document Scope and Executive Summary

This HR reference document (HR-POLICY-1395) outlines the core policies, standard operating procedures, and compliance guidelines for the Human Resources department. The purpose of this document is to provide a centralized repository of HR-related information, ensuring consistency and fairness in the management of employee matters.

The scope of this document includes all aspects of HR operations, including recruitment, employment, benefits, performance management, and separation processes. This document serves as a reference guide for HR staff, managers, and employees, providing clear procedures and guidelines for the resolution of common HR-related issues.

### 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

#### Recruitment and Hiring

* All job openings are posted on the company's internal job board and external career website.
* Job descriptions are reviewed and approved by hiring managers and department heads before posting.
* Application materials are evaluated based on qualifications, experience, and fit for the role.

#### Employment and Benefits

* All employees are eligible for comprehensive benefits package, including health, dental, vision, and life insurance.
* Employees are entitled to paid time off (vacation, sick leave, and personal days) according to company policies.
* Employee performance evaluations are conducted annually, with opportunities for growth and development.

#### Performance Management

* Performance goals and objectives are established at the beginning of each quarter.
* Regular check-ins and feedback sessions are held between managers and employees.
* Annual performance evaluations are conducted, with possible adjustments to salary or title based on performance.

#### Separation and Exit Procedures

* All employee terminations are documented in writing and communicated to HR and management.
* Employees are entitled to a minimum of two weeks' notice, unless otherwise negotiated.
* Exit interviews are conducted to gather feedback and improve future processes.

### 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

#### Compliance Monitoring

* The HR department is responsible for ensuring compliance with all applicable laws, regulations, and company policies.
* Regular audits and reviews are conducted to identify potential non-compliance issues.
* Corrective actions are taken in response to any identified non-compliance.

#### Penalties and Disciplinary Actions

* Verbal warnings are issued for minor infractions or first-time offenses.
* Written warnings are issued for more serious offenses, with possible suspension or termination following a third written warning.
* All disciplinary actions are documented and communicated to HR and management.

#### Operational Governance

* The HR department is responsible for maintaining accurate and up-to-date employee records.
* Employee data is protected in accordance with company privacy policies and applicable laws.
* Regular training sessions are conducted on HR-related topics, including compliance and best practices.
