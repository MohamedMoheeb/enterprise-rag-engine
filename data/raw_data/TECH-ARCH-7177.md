# TECH-ARCH-7177: Architecture Reference Document
## Department: Architecture
## Author: Diane Marquez
## Effective Date: 2022-09-04

### Document Scope and Executive Summary
This document serves as the primary reference for the Architecture department, outlining core policies, standard operating procedures, and guidelines for all architectural design and development activities. The purpose of this document is to ensure consistency, efficiency, and quality in our architecture practices while maintaining compliance with relevant regulations and industry standards.

### Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

#### 1.1. Design Principles
The Architecture department adheres to the following design principles:

* Modularity: All architectural designs should be modular, allowing for easy scalability and maintainability.
* Reusability: Designs should prioritize reusability to minimize duplication of effort and improve overall efficiency.
* Scalability: Architectures must be designed with scalability in mind to accommodate future growth and changes.

#### 1.2. Development Process
The Architecture department follows a rigorous development process, which includes:

* Requirements Gathering: Thoroughly gathering and documenting project requirements.
* Design: Creating detailed design specifications and prototypes.
* Implementation: Developing and testing the architecture.
* Testing: Conducting thorough testing to ensure compliance with design principles and functional requirements.
* Deployment: Deploying the architecture in production.

#### 1.3. Change Management
The Architecture department follows a formal change management process, which includes:

* Request for Change (RFC): Submitting requests for changes to existing architectures or introducing new ones.
* Review: Conducting thorough reviews of proposed changes to ensure compliance with design principles and standards.
* Approval: Obtaining approval from relevant stakeholders before implementing changes.

### Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

#### 2.1. Compliance Monitoring
The Architecture department will conduct regular monitoring and audits to ensure compliance with this document's policies and procedures.

#### 2.2. Penalties for Non-Compliance
 Failure to comply with the guidelines outlined in this document may result in:

* Verbal warnings for first-time offenders.
* Written warnings for repeat offenders.
* Mandatory training or retraining for persistent non-compliance.
* Termination of employment for severe and repeated violations.

#### 2.3. Operational Governance
The Architecture department will maintain accurate records, reports, and documentation to ensure transparency and accountability in all architectural design and development activities.

This document supersedes all previous versions and is effective as of the Effective Date stated above. Any updates or revisions will be documented and disseminated through official channels.
