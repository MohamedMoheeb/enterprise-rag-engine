# 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

## Architecture Design Principles

The Architecture department adheres to the following design principles:

* **Modularity**: Designs should be modular, with clear interfaces between components.
* **Scalability**: Designs should be scalable, allowing for easy addition or removal of components as needed.
* **Reusability**: Designs should promote reusability by minimizing dependencies and promoting code sharing.

## Architecture Review Process

The architecture review process involves the following steps:

1. **Design Submission**: The architect submits their design proposal to the Architecture department for review.
2. **Initial Review**: The Architecture department conducts an initial review of the design proposal, providing feedback on feasibility, scalability, and reusability.
3. **Iteration**: The architect refines their design based on feedback from the Architecture department.
4. **Final Review**: The Architecture department conducts a final review of the design proposal, ensuring it meets company standards and best practices.
5. **Approval**: The design is approved for implementation.

## Change Management Process

The change management process involves the following steps:

1. **Change Request**: A team member submits a request to make changes to an existing architecture design.
2. **Impact Assessment**: The Architecture department assesses the impact of the proposed changes on the overall system and stakeholders.
3. **Design Update**: The architect updates their design proposal to reflect the requested changes.
4. **Review and Approval**: The updated design is reviewed and approved by the Architecture department.

# 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

## Compliance Monitoring

The Architecture department is responsible for monitoring compliance with architecture standards and best practices. This includes:

* Conducting regular audits of architecture designs
* Providing training and guidance on architecture principles and standards
* Ensuring that all stakeholders understand the importance of adhering to architecture guidelines

## Penalties

 Failure to comply with architecture standards and best practices may result in penalties, including:

* **Design Rejection**: The design is rejected, requiring rework or a new submission.
* **Project Delay**: The project is delayed due to non-compliance with architecture guidelines.
* **Financial Consequences**: The company may incur additional costs due to non-compliance.

## Operational Governance

The Architecture department operates under the following governance structure:

* **Architecture Committee**: The committee is responsible for setting architecture standards, reviewing design proposals, and ensuring compliance with best practices.
* **Architecture Lead**: The lead is responsible for overseeing the Architecture department, providing guidance on architecture principles and standards, and ensuring that all stakeholders understand their roles and responsibilities.

By following this document, the Architecture department will ensure that our company's products and services are designed and developed in a way that meets our customers' needs, while also promoting scalability, reusability, and maintainability.
