## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1. Architecture Principles

The following principles guide architectural decisions within the Architecture department:

* Modularity: Design for scalability, flexibility, and maintainability
* Abstraction: Hide complexity with simple interfaces and APIs
* Adaptability: Prepare for change by designing for evolution
* Consistency: Ensure consistency in design and architecture across systems and applications
* Efficiency: Optimize system performance, memory usage, and processing power

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1. Compliance Monitoring

The Architecture department will monitor compliance with architecture principles, design standards, and development life cycle procedures through:

* Regular code reviews and audits
* Quarterly architecture committee meetings to review and discuss architectural decisions
* Annual architecture governance reports to the executive team

### 3.2. Penalties

Failure to comply with architecture principles, design standards, or development life cycle procedures may result in penalties, including but not limited to:

* Delayed project timelines
* Additional training or education requirements
* Limited access to resources and tools
* Disciplinary action for repeat offenders

### 3.3. Operational Governance

The Architecture department will operate under the following governance structure:

* Architecture committee: A cross-functional team responsible for reviewing and approving architectural decisions, ensuring compliance with architecture principles and design standards.
* Architecture leads: Responsible for guiding architectural decisions, providing technical guidance, and coordinating with other departments.
* Architecture champions: Enthusiasts who promote architecture best practices within their respective teams or departments.

By following this Architecture Reference Document, the Architecture department will ensure the successful development of architectural solutions that meet business needs while maintaining compliance with established policies and procedures.
