## 1. Document Scope and Executive Summary

This document outlines the core policies, standard operating procedures, and compliance monitoring framework for [Company Name]'s Human Resources (HR) department. The purpose of this document is to provide a comprehensive guide for HR staff, employees, and management on the principles, rules, and guidelines governing HR practices.

The scope of this document includes all aspects of HR operations, including employee recruitment, hiring, onboarding, benefits administration, compensation, performance management, employee relations, conflict resolution, and employee termination. This document also outlines the procedures for handling sensitive HR matters, such as harassment, discrimination, and workplace violence.

## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Employee Classification and Job Descriptions

* All employees will be classified into one of three categories: exempt, non-exempt, or managerial.
* Each job will have a clearly defined job description outlining the duties, responsibilities, and performance expectations.
* Job descriptions will be reviewed and updated annually.

### 2.2 Employee Recruitment and Hiring

* The HR department will be responsible for posting job openings on company-approved job boards and social media platforms.
* All job applicants must complete an online application form, including a resume and cover letter.
* Interviews will be conducted by a minimum of two interviewers, with one being the hiring manager.
* A comprehensive background check will be performed on all new hires.

### 2.3 Employee Onboarding and Orientation

* New employees will receive a comprehensive onboarding package, including company policies, benefits information, and job-specific training.
* The HR department will ensure that all new employees complete a mandatory orientation program within their first week of employment.

### 2.4 Performance Management and Evaluation

* Employees will be evaluated annually, with clear performance expectations outlined in their individual performance goals.
* Managers will conduct regular feedback sessions with employees to discuss progress, goals, and areas for improvement.
* The HR department will maintain a record of all employee evaluations and performance goals.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

* The HR department will be responsible for monitoring compliance with company policies, laws, and regulations.
* Regular audits will be conducted to ensure compliance with HR-related processes and procedures.
* The HR department will maintain a record of all compliance monitoring activities.

### 3.2 Penalties and Consequences

* Failure to comply with company policies or procedures may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination.
* Employees found to have engaged in misconduct or unethical behavior may be subject to penalties, such as suspension or termination.

### 3.3 Operational Governance

* The HR department will maintain a centralized database of all HR-related data, including employee records, performance evaluations, and compliance monitoring activities.
* The HR department will ensure that all HR operations are conducted in accordance with company policies, laws, and regulations.
* The HR department will provide regular training and support to HR staff on HR-related procedures and best practices.
