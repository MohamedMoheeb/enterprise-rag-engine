## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Design Principles

* The Architecture department will adhere to the following design principles:
	+ Separation of Concerns (SoC): Components should be designed to handle specific tasks, reducing complexity and improving maintainability.
	+ Single Responsibility Principle (SRP): Each component should have a single responsibility, making it easier to maintain and update.
	+ Abstraction: Higher-level abstractions will be used to encapsulate complex logic and reduce coupling.

### 2.2 Architecture Review Board (ARB)

* The ARB will review and approve all architectural designs, ensuring they align with company policies and best practices.
* The ARB will consist of:
	+ Chief Architect
	+ Technical Leads from various departments
	+ Representatives from the Business and IT teams

### 2.3 Code Quality and Standards

* All code will be written in accordance with the company's coding standards and guidelines.
* Code reviews will be conducted by peers or mentors to ensure adherence to these standards.

### 2.4 Change Management

* The Architecture department will maintain a change management process to track, approve, and implement changes to our architecture.
* Changes will be evaluated based on their impact on the overall system, business, and stakeholders.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Regulatory Compliance

* The Architecture department will ensure compliance with relevant regulatory requirements, such as GDPR, HIPAA, and PCI-DSS.
* Regular audits and assessments will be conducted to identify potential compliance issues.

### 3.2 Non-Compliance Penalties

* Failure to comply with company policies or regulatory requirements may result in:
	+ Verbal warnings
	+ Written warnings
	+ Suspension of architecture-related activities
	+ Termination of employment

### 3.3 Operational Governance

* The Architecture department will maintain a governance structure, including:
	+ Regular meetings with stakeholders and the ARB to discuss architectural decisions and changes.
	+ Quarterly reports on architecture-related activities and performance metrics.

By following this reference document, the Architecture department will ensure that our technology solutions meet business needs while maintaining compliance with company policies and regulatory requirements.
