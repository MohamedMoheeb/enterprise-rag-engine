# TECH-ARCH-6879: Architecture Reference Document
## Department: Architecture
## Author: Jeremy Acosta
## Effective Date: 2021-06-15

### Document Scope and Executive Summary

This Architecture Reference Document (ARD) outlines the policies, procedures, and best practices for the Architecture department at [Company Name]. The purpose of this document is to provide a centralized resource for architects, engineers, and other stakeholders involved in the design and development of our company's products and services.

The ARD aims to ensure that all architecture-related activities are conducted in a consistent, efficient, and scalable manner. This document applies to all employees, contractors, and partners involved in the Architecture department.

### 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

#### 2.1 Design Principles and Guidelines

* Follow [Company Name]'s design principles and guidelines for architecture development.
* Ensure that all designs meet the company's technical and non-technical requirements.
* Document all design decisions and rationales.

#### 2.2 Architecture Development Process

* Follow a structured approach for designing, developing, and testing architectures.
* Conduct regular peer reviews and approvals for architecture documentation.
* Ensure that all architectures are thoroughly tested and validated before implementation.

#### 2.3 Code Quality and Standards

* Adhere to [Company Name]'s coding standards and best practices for software development.
* Ensure that all code is properly documented, tested, and maintained.
* Conduct regular code reviews and refactorings as needed.

### 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

#### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

* Regularly review and monitor architecture-related activities to ensure compliance with this ARD.
* Identify and address any non-compliance issues promptly.
* Document all compliance monitoring activities and results.

#### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

* Failure to comply with the ARD may result in:
	+ Verbal warnings
	+ Written warnings
	+ Suspension or termination of architecture-related activities
	+ Performance improvement plans

#### 3.3 Operational Governance

* Establish clear roles and responsibilities for architecture-related activities.
* Define and maintain a centralized architecture repository (CAR) for storing and tracking architectures.
* Conduct regular architecture governance meetings to discuss progress, issues, and best practices.

### Conclusion

This Architecture Reference Document provides a comprehensive framework for the Architecture department at [Company Name]. By following this document's policies, procedures, and guidelines, we can ensure that our company's products and services are designed and developed in a consistent, efficient, and scalable manner.
