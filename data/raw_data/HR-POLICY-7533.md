Scope:

The goal of this document is to ensure a consistent and fair application of HR policies, procedures, and guidelines across the organization. ## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Employee Classification and Job Descriptions

* All employees will be classified as either exempt or non-exempt based on their job responsibilities and FLSA guidelines.
* Job descriptions will be reviewed and updated annually to ensure accuracy and relevance.

### 2.2 Time-Off Requests and Scheduling

* Employees are entitled to [X] days of paid time off per year, including vacation, sick leave, and personal days.
* Time-off requests must be submitted [X] days in advance, unless due to an emergency or unforeseen circumstances.
* Managers will review and approve/deny requests based on business needs and employee availability.

### 2.3 Performance Evaluations and Goal Setting

* Annual performance evaluations will be conducted by managers to assess employee performance and set goals for the upcoming year.
* Employees will receive written feedback and a clear action plan outlining areas for improvement and opportunities for growth.

### 2.4 Confidentiality, Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs), and Intellectual Property

* All employees are required to sign an NDA upon hiring or as needed for specific projects or roles.
* Confidential information and intellectual property will be protected in accordance with company policies and applicable laws.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

* HR will conduct regular audits and reviews to ensure compliance with company policies, regulations, and industry standards.
* Employees are expected to report any non-compliance or potential violations to their supervisor or HR representative immediately.

### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

* First-time offenders: verbal warning
* Second-time offenders: written warning
* Third-time offenders: disciplinary action up to and including termination

### 3.3 Operational Governance

* HR will maintain accurate records of employee data, including personnel files, time-off requests, and performance evaluations.
* Managers are responsible for ensuring that their teams comply with company policies and procedures.

This document is effective as of the Effective Date listed above. Any updates or revisions will be documented and communicated to employees through regular training sessions, email notifications, or other means deemed necessary by HR.
