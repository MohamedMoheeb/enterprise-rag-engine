## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Architecture Development Process

The architecture development process consists of the following stages:

* **Requirements gathering**: Identify business needs and technical requirements
* **Architecture design**: Develop high-level and detailed designs
* **Implementation planning**: Plan and coordinate implementation efforts
* **Deployment and testing**: Deploy and test architectures
* **Maintenance and update**: Monitor and update architectures as needed

### 2.2 Change Management Process

The change management process involves:

* **Request submission**: Submit changes to the architecture committee for review
* **Review and approval**: Review and approve or reject changes based on architectural standards and business objectives
* **Implementation planning**: Plan and coordinate implementation efforts for approved changes

### 2.3 Architecture Governance Structure

The architecture governance structure includes:

* **Architecture committee**: Responsible for reviewing, approving, and maintaining architectures
* **Technical leads**: Provide technical expertise and guidance
* **Business stakeholders**: Represent business needs and priorities

### 2.4 Communication and Collaboration

Effective communication and collaboration are crucial for successful architecture development and maintenance. The following guidelines apply:

* **Regular meetings**: Hold regular meetings with stakeholders to ensure alignment and progress tracking
* **Documentation**: Maintain accurate and up-to-date documentation of architectures, designs, and implementation plans
* **Feedback mechanisms**: Establish feedback mechanisms for stakeholders to provide input and suggestions

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

The architecture committee is responsible for monitoring compliance with the architecture framework and SOPs. This includes:

* **Regular audits**: Conduct regular audits to ensure adherence to established guidelines
* **Corrective actions**: Implement corrective actions when non-compliance is identified

### 3.2 Penalties

In cases of non-compliance, penalties may be imposed as follows:

* **Warning**: Issue a warning for minor deviations from established guidelines
* **Re-training**: Require re-training or additional education for moderate deviations
* **Penalty**: Impose a penalty (e.g., suspension or termination) for significant deviations

### 3.3 Operational Governance

To ensure effective operational governance, the following procedures apply:

* **Reporting and metrics**: Establish reporting and metric tracking to monitor architecture performance and compliance
* **Incident response**: Develop incident response plans to address unexpected events or issues
* **Continuous improvement**: Foster a culture of continuous improvement by encouraging feedback and implementing changes as needed
