# TECH-ARCH-5393: Architecture Reference Document
## Department: Architecture
## Author: Catherine Case DDS
## Effective Date: 2026-04-23

### Document Scope and Executive Summary

This document serves as the primary reference for the Architecture department, outlining core policies, standard operating procedures, and compliance monitoring guidelines. The purpose of this document is to establish a unified framework for architectural design, development, and deployment across the organization.

The Architecture department is responsible for designing, building, and maintaining the company's technical infrastructure. This includes creating and implementing architectural designs, developing technical specifications, and ensuring compliance with industry standards and regulations.

### Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

#### 1.1. Design Principles

* The Architecture department shall adhere to the following design principles:
	+ Scalability: Designs must be scalable to accommodate future growth.
	+ Security: Designs must prioritize security and data protection.
	+ Interoperability: Designs must ensure seamless integration with existing systems.
	+ Maintainability: Designs must be easy to maintain and update.

#### 1.2. Development Process

* The Architecture department shall follow the following development process:
	1. Requirements gathering: Gather requirements from stakeholders and customers.
	2. Design creation: Create architectural designs based on gathered requirements.
	3. Technical specification development: Develop technical specifications for implementation.
	4. Implementation: Implement designed solutions, ensuring compliance with technical specifications.
	5. Testing and validation: Conduct thorough testing and validation to ensure solution meets requirements.

#### 1.3. Change Management

* The Architecture department shall implement the following change management process:
	1. Request submission: Submit requests for changes or updates to existing designs.
	2. Review and approval: Review and approve requests based on design principles and impact analysis.
	3. Implementation: Implement approved changes, ensuring compliance with technical specifications.

### Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

#### 2.1. Compliance Monitoring

* The Architecture department shall conduct regular compliance monitoring to ensure adherence to:
	+ Industry standards (e.g., ISO 27001, HIPAA)
	+ Regulatory requirements (e.g., GDPR, CCPA)
	+ Company policies and procedures

#### 2.2. Penalties for Non-Compliance

* In the event of non-compliance, the Architecture department shall impose the following penalties:
	+ Warning: First-time offenders will receive a written warning.
	+ Suspension: Repeated offenses may result in suspension from architectural design activities.

#### 2.3. Operational Governance

* The Architecture department shall establish and maintain an operational governance framework to ensure:
	+ Effective communication among stakeholders
	+ Timely issue resolution
	+ Continuous improvement of processes and procedures
