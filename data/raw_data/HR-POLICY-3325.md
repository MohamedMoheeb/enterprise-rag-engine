## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Employee Classification and Hiring

* Definition: [Company Name] employs individuals as full-time or part-time employees, contractors, interns, or volunteers.
* Policy:
	+ Employees are classified based on their job responsibilities, work schedule, and compensation package.
	+ The hiring process includes a thorough background check, reference verification, and a review of the candidate's qualifications.

### 2.2 Employee Performance Management

* Policy:
	+ Regular performance evaluations are conducted to assess employee achievements, areas for improvement, and career development opportunities.
	+ Employees have access to training programs and resources to enhance their skills and knowledge.

### 2.3 Compensation and Benefits

* Policy:
	+ The company offers a competitive compensation package that includes salary, bonuses, and benefits (health insurance, retirement plans, etc.).
	+ Salary increases are based on performance evaluations and industry standards.

### 2.4 Workplace Conduct and Harassment Prevention

* Policy:
	+ [Company Name] is committed to maintaining a workplace free from harassment, discrimination, and bullying.
	+ Employees are expected to respect each other's differences, maintain confidentiality, and refrain from engaging in abusive or offensive behavior.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

* The HR department is responsible for ensuring compliance with relevant laws, regulations, and industry standards.
* Regular audits and monitoring are conducted to identify and address any non-compliance issues.

### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

* Failure to comply with HR policies or procedures may result in disciplinary actions, including verbal warnings, written warnings, suspensions, or terminations.
* Employees who violate company policies or procedures may be subject to legal action.

### 3.3 Operational Governance

* The HR department is responsible for maintaining accurate and up-to-date records of employee data, performance evaluations, and compliance monitoring results.
* The HR department works closely with other departments (e.g., Legal, Finance) to ensure seamless operational integration and effective governance.
