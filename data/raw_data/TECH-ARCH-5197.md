## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Design Principles

The Architecture department follows a set of design principles that guide the development of architecture solutions. These principles include:

* scalability and reliability
* security and integrity
* maintainability and flexibility
* performance and efficiency
* usability and accessibility

### 2.2 Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC)

The SDLC process includes the following stages:

1. Requirements gathering and analysis
2. Design and prototyping
3. Development and testing
4. Deployment and maintenance

### 2.3 Architecture Review Board (ARB)

The ARB is responsible for reviewing and approving architecture designs, ensuring compliance with company policies and standards.

### 2.4 Change Management Process

The change management process involves the following steps:

1. Request submission
2. Assessment and prioritization
3. Design and implementation
4. Testing and validation
5. Deployment and monitoring

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

Compliance monitoring includes regular reviews of architecture designs, development processes, and deployment practices to ensure adherence to company policies and standards.

### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

Penalties for non-compliance with architecture policies and procedures include:

* Written warnings
* Suspension or termination of privileges
* Mandatory training or re-training
* Financial penalties or fines

### 3.3 Operational Governance

Operational governance includes the following key performance indicators (KPIs):

1. Architecture design quality and effectiveness
2. Development cycle time and efficiency
3. Deployment reliability and uptime
4. Maintenance and support response times and resolution rates

By following this reference document, the Architecture department can ensure that its architecture solutions meet the company's needs, comply with relevant regulations and standards, and operate efficiently and effectively.
