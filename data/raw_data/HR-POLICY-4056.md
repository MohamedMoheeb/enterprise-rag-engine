## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Employee Relations

* Harassment Policy: [Company Name] prohibits harassment in the workplace and provides a reporting mechanism for employees to report incidents.
* Code of Conduct: Employees are expected to adhere to the company's code of conduct, which includes guidelines on professional behavior, confidentiality, and conflict of interest.

### 2.2 Compensation and Benefits

* Salary Administration Policy: The company follows a formal salary administration process to ensure fair and equitable compensation practices.
* Employee Benefits Package: The company offers a comprehensive benefits package, including health insurance, retirement plans, and paid time off.

### 2.3 Performance Management

* Performance Evaluation Process: Employees undergo regular performance evaluations to assess their job performance and set goals for improvement.
* Corrective Action Procedure: A formal process is in place for addressing underperformance or misconduct, including progressive disciplinary action up to termination.

### 2.4 Recruitment and Onboarding

* Job Requisition Process: HR manages the job requisition process, ensuring that open positions are properly posted and filled in a timely manner.
* New Hire Orientation: All new hires participate in an orientation program, which includes training on company policies, benefits, and expectations.

### 2.5 Employee Development and Separation

* Training and Development Opportunities: The company offers various training programs and opportunities for employee development and growth.
* Separation Policy: A formal separation process is in place, including notification requirements, exit interviews, and reference checks.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

* Regular audits and reviews are conducted to ensure compliance with company policies, laws, and regulations.
* HR ensures that all employees receive training on relevant compliance issues, such as equal employment opportunity, data privacy, and anti-bribery laws.

### 3.2 Penalties

* Failure to comply with company policies or laws may result in disciplinary action up to termination.
* Repeated violations of company policies or laws may lead to more severe penalties, including legal action.

### 3.3 Operational Governance

* HR is responsible for maintaining accurate and up-to-date records of employee data, performance, and attendance.
* The department ensures that all employees are aware of their roles and responsibilities in supporting the organization's goals and objectives.

By following this document, HR aims to ensure a positive and productive work environment, while also promoting compliance with relevant laws and regulations.
