## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures
### 2.1 Architectural Principles
The Architecture department follows these core architectural principles:

* Separation of Concerns (SoC): Divide systems into independent components to promote maintainability, scalability, and reusability.
* Single Responsibility Principle (SRP): Assign a single responsibility to each component or module to reduce complexity and improve maintainability.
* Don't Repeat Yourself (DRY): Avoid duplicating code or logic to promote reuse, simplicity, and efficiency.

### 2.2 Architecture Review Process
The architecture review process involves the following steps:

1. **Architecture Request**: Submit a request for architectural guidance or review through the company's issue tracking system.
2. **Initial Assessment**: The Architecture team conducts an initial assessment of the request to determine its scope, complexity, and potential impact on the organization.
3. **Design Review**: Conduct a design review with stakeholders to validate assumptions, clarify requirements, and ensure alignment with architectural principles and standards.
4. **Approved Design**: Approve the proposed design and provide feedback for implementation.

### 2.3 Architecture Standards
The Architecture department adheres to the following standards:

* Technology Stack: Utilize [Company Name]'s approved technology stack for software development.
* Frameworks and Libraries: Use established frameworks and libraries to promote reusability, efficiency, and maintainability.
* Coding Guidelines: Follow established coding guidelines for consistency, readability, and maintainability.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance
### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

The Architecture department will:

* Conduct regular compliance checks on architecture-related activities to ensure adherence to company standards and policies.
* Monitor architectural designs, code reviews, and implementation for alignment with approved principles and standards.

### 3.2 Penalties

In case of non-compliance, the following penalties may be imposed:

* Warning: A written warning to the individual or team responsible for the non-compliance.
* Fine: A fine equivalent to one day's pay for each instance of non-compliance.
* Re-education: Mandatory re-education on company standards and policies.

### 3.3 Operational Governance

The Architecture department will:

* Establish clear communication channels with stakeholders, teams, and departments.
* Develop and maintain a comprehensive architecture roadmap to ensure alignment with business objectives.
* Provide training and support for architects and other teams involved in software development and deployment.
