## 1. Document Scope and Executive Summary

This reference document outlines the core policies, standard operating procedures (SOPs), and operational governance for the Architecture department. The purpose of this document is to provide a comprehensive guide for architects, engineers, and other stakeholders involved in designing and implementing architectural solutions within our organization.

The scope of this document includes:

* Defining the role and responsibilities of the Architecture department
* Establishing core policies and procedures for architecture development, review, and approval
* Outlining compliance monitoring and penalty procedures
* Providing guidance on operational governance and decision-making processes

## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1. Architecture Development Process

The architecture development process involves the following stages:

1. **Initiation**: Request for architecture services is submitted to the Architecture department.
2. **Analysis**: Architects conduct a thorough analysis of the business requirements and existing infrastructure.
3. **Design**: Architects design a proposed architecture solution, considering factors such as scalability, security, and maintainability.
4. **Review**: The proposed architecture solution is reviewed by internal stakeholders and external experts (if applicable).
5. **Approval**: The final architecture solution is approved or rejected based on the review outcome.
6. **Implementation**: The approved architecture solution is implemented in accordance with established procedures.

### 2.2. Architecture Review and Approval Process

The architecture review and approval process involves the following stages:

1. **Request for Review**: An architect submits a proposed architecture solution for review.
2. **Initial Screening**: A designated reviewer conducts an initial screening to ensure the proposal meets basic criteria (e.g., compliance with organizational standards).
3. **Detailed Review**: The proposed architecture solution is reviewed in detail by internal stakeholders and external experts (if applicable).
4. **Approval or Rejection**: The final architecture solution is approved or rejected based on the review outcome.

### 2.3. Change Management Process

The change management process involves the following stages:

1. **Request for Change**: A request for a change to an existing architecture solution is submitted.
2. **Assessment**: The requested change is assessed against established criteria (e.g., impact on security, scalability, and maintainability).
3. **Design**: A proposed design for the change is developed, considering factors such as feasibility and cost.
4. **Review**: The proposed change is reviewed by internal stakeholders and external experts (if applicable).
5. **Approval or Rejection**: The requested change is approved or rejected based on the review outcome.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1. Compliance Monitoring

The Architecture department will monitor compliance with established policies and procedures through regular audits and reviews.

### 3.2. Penalties for Non-Compliance

In cases of non-compliance, the following penalties may apply:

* **Minor Infractions**: A warning or minor fine.
* **Serious Infractions**: A formal reprimand, suspension, or termination of employment.

### 3.3. Operational Governance

The Architecture department will operate in accordance with established organizational policies and procedures. Decision-making processes will be transparent, fair, and based on objective criteria.
