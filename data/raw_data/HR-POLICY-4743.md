## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Employee Recruitment and Onboarding

Policy: Ensure timely and effective recruitment processes, including job postings, candidate screening, and new hire onboarding.

Procedure:

* Job postings are created according to company guidelines.
* Candidates are screened based on qualifications and experience.
* New hires complete an orientation program within the first two weeks of employment.
* All necessary paperwork is completed and filed in a timely manner.

### 2.2 Employee Performance Management

Policy: Foster open communication, transparency, and accountability through regular performance evaluations and goal-setting processes.

Procedure:

* Employees receive regular feedback and performance evaluations.
* Goals and objectives are established based on individual and team performance.
* Development plans are created to address skill gaps and areas for improvement.
* All performance-related documents are maintained in a secure database.

### 2.3 Employee Compensation and Benefits

Policy: Ensure fair and competitive compensation packages, including salary, bonuses, and benefits.

Procedure:

* Salary ranges are reviewed annually based on market data.
* Bonus structures are established to incentivize high-performance employees.
* Employee benefits, such as health insurance, retirement plans, and paid time off, are offered in accordance with company policies.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Regulatory Compliance

Policy: Ensure compliance with relevant laws, regulations, and industry standards governing HR operations.

Procedure:

* Regular audits are conducted to ensure compliance with regulatory requirements.
* Employees receive training on relevant laws and regulations.
* Violations of compliance policies may result in disciplinary action up to termination.

### 3.2 Confidentiality and Data Protection

Policy: Protect employee personal data and maintain confidentiality in all HR-related activities.

Procedure:

* Employee data is stored securely and access is restricted to authorized personnel.
* All HR documents are maintained in a secure database with controlled access.
* Violations of confidentiality policies may result in disciplinary action up to termination.

### 3.3 Operational Governance

Policy: Establish clear guidelines for HR operations, including budgeting, resource allocation, and project management.

Procedure:

* Annual budgets are established based on departmental needs and priorities.
* Resources are allocated effectively to support HR initiatives and projects.
* Project managers are responsible for ensuring timely completion of HR-related tasks and initiatives.
