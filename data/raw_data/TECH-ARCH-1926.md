## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Architecture Framework

The architecture framework outlines the principles, patterns, and guidelines for designing buildings that meet [Company Name]'s business objectives and stakeholder requirements. The framework includes:

* **Architecture Principles:** Guiding design decisions based on scalability, reliability, security, and maintainability
* **Patterns and Guidelines:** Standardized approaches to common architectural challenges

### 2.2 Design Standards

The design standards outline the visual and aesthetic guidelines for building designs, including:

* **Visual Identity:** Branding elements, color schemes, and typography
* **Design Elements:** Fonts, imagery, and graphics
* **Layout and Composition:** Page layout, hierarchy, and whitespace management

### 2.3 Design Process

The design process outlines the steps involved in designing a building, including:

* **Concept Development:** Initial idea generation and exploration
* **Design Development:** Refining designs based on stakeholder feedback and technical requirements
* **Implementation:** Building construction and deployment

### 2.4 Change Management

Change management procedures govern how changes are made to existing buildings or architectural designs, including:

* **Request for Change (RFC):** Submitting change requests with justification and impact assessment
* **Change Review:** Evaluating the feasibility and impact of proposed changes
* **Implementation:** Executing approved changes and documenting lessons learned

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

Compliance monitoring ensures that architectural designs meet [Company Name]'s standards, regulations, and industry best practices. This includes:

* **Audits and Inspections:** Regular reviews of design documents and physical buildings
* **Training and Education:** Providing architects with ongoing training and education on compliance requirements

### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

Penalties for non-compliance with architectural standards include:

* **Warning Letters:** Initial notifications of non-compliance
* **Design Revisions:** Mandatory revisions to address identified issues
* **Financial Penalties:** Fines or deductions from bonuses for repeated non-compliance

### 3.3 Operational Governance

Operational governance ensures that the Architecture department operates effectively and efficiently, including:

* **Roles and Responsibilities:** Clear definitions of roles and responsibilities within the department
* **Meetings and Communications:** Regular meetings and communication channels to facilitate collaboration and decision-making
* **Performance Metrics:** Tracking key performance indicators (KPIs) for measuring architectural success
