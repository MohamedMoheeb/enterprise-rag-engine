* SOPs for architecture project management, design, and delivery

## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Architecture Design Principles

* The architecture shall be designed to support the organization's strategic objectives.
* The architecture shall be scalable, secure, and reliable.
* The architecture shall adhere to industry-recognized standards and best practices.

### 2.2 Project Management SOPs

* Project initiation: A project charter shall be created, outlining the scope, goals, and timelines for the project.
* Project planning: A detailed project plan shall be developed, including milestones, dependencies, and resource allocation.
* Project execution: The architecture team shall execute the project plan, with regular status updates and progress reporting.

### 2.3 Design and Delivery SOPs

* Architecture design: Architects shall follow a structured design process, including requirements gathering, conceptual design, detailed design, and testing.
* Code reviews: All code shall be reviewed by at least one other architect to ensure adherence to architectural principles and best practices.
* Testing and quality assurance: Thorough testing and quality assurance procedures shall be performed before project delivery.

### 2.4 Change Management SOPs

* Request for change: A formal request for change shall be submitted, outlining the proposed changes, impact analysis, and justification.
* Change evaluation: The architecture team shall evaluate the requested change, considering factors such as risk, cost, and business value.
* Change implementation: Approved changes shall be implemented according to the project plan, with regular status updates.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

* Regular audits and reviews shall be performed to ensure compliance with organizational policies and external regulations.
* Architecture team members shall participate in training and awareness programs to stay up-to-date with changing regulatory requirements.

### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

* Failure to comply with architectural principles, SOPs, or regulatory requirements may result in disciplinary action, including warnings, suspensions, or termination of employment.
* Repeated non-compliance may lead to project delays, budget overruns, or even project cancellation.

### 3.3 Operational Governance

* The architecture team shall be accountable for the delivery and maintenance of architectural solutions.
* The architecture team shall maintain accurate records of project activities, including design decisions, testing results, and change requests.
* The architecture team shall participate in continuous improvement initiatives to refine processes and improve quality.

By following this Architecture Reference Document, the Architecture department at [Company Name] can ensure that our organization's infrastructure is designed and developed with integrity, reliability, and scalability.
