## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Employee Conduct and Behavior

* All employees are expected to adhere to the company's Code of Conduct, which outlines acceptable behavior in the workplace.
* Employees must respect their colleagues' rights, dignity, and privacy.
* Any incidents or concerns related to employee conduct or behavior should be reported to HR or management.

### 2.2 Recruitment and Hiring

* All job postings will include a clear description of the role, responsibilities, and required qualifications.
* The hiring process will involve multiple stakeholders, including HR, managers, and subject matter experts.
* Offers will only be made after thorough reference checks and verification of candidate credentials.

### 2.3 Employee Records and Data Management

* All employee records will be maintained in accordance with relevant data protection laws and regulations.
* Access to employee records will be restricted to authorized personnel only.
* Employees have the right to review their own personal records upon request.

### 2.4 Performance Management and Development

* The company will maintain a performance management system that includes regular feedback, goal-setting, and evaluations.
* All employees will participate in mandatory training and development programs to enhance their skills and knowledge.
* Managers are responsible for providing constructive feedback and coaching to improve employee performance.

### 2.5 Leave and Absence Management

* The company will provide paid annual leave, sick leave, and family and parental leave as per applicable laws and regulations.
* Employees must notify their manager or HR representative in advance of taking leave or reporting an absence.
* Managers are responsible for ensuring that employees' workloads are managed during periods of leave or absence.

### 2.6 Workplace Health and Safety

* The company will maintain a safe and healthy work environment, free from hazards and risks.
* Employees must report any incidents, near-miss events, or workplace accidents to HR or management immediately.
* Managers are responsible for conducting regular safety inspections and providing training on relevant safety procedures.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

* The company will conduct regular audits and reviews to ensure compliance with applicable laws, regulations, and policies.
* HR personnel will be responsible for monitoring employee records, attendance, and performance data.
* Managers are expected to maintain accurate records of employee activities, including training, leave, and absences.

### 3.2 Penalties

* Employees found to have violated company policies or procedures may face disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment.
* The company reserves the right to impose penalties for non-compliance with laws, regulations, or policies.

### 3.3 Operational Governance

* HR personnel will be responsible for ensuring that all employee-related matters are processed in accordance with established procedures and guidelines.
* Managers are expected to maintain open communication channels with their employees and address any concerns or issues promptly.
* The company will provide regular training and development opportunities to enhance the skills and knowledge of HR personnel, managers, and employees.

By following the policies, SOPs, and procedures outlined in this document, [Company Name] can ensure a positive work environment, promote employee engagement, and achieve its business objectives.
