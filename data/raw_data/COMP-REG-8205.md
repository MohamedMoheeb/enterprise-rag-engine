# COMP-REG-8205: Compliance Reference Document
## Department: Compliance
## Author: Diamond Davis
## Effective Date: 2026-05-10

### Document Scope and Executive Summary

This compliance reference document (CRD) outlines the core policies, standard operating procedures (SOPs), and operational governance guidelines for the Enterprise Compliance department. The primary objective of this document is to provide a comprehensive framework for ensuring adherence to regulatory requirements, industry standards, and internal policies.

The scope of this CRD includes:

* Overview of compliance responsibilities
* Core policies governing business operations
* Standard operating procedures for managing compliance risks
* Guidelines for monitoring and reporting compliance performance
* Penalties and disciplinary actions for non-compliance

### 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

#### Policy 1: Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Combating the Financing of Terrorism (CFT)

* Definition: The enterprise prohibits transactions involving proceeds from illegal activities or financing of terrorism.
* Procedure:
	+ Conduct ongoing customer due diligence for high-risk customers
	+ Implement enhanced due diligence for new customers with a risk profile above a certain threshold
	+ Monitor suspicious transactions and report to the Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU)

#### Policy 2: Data Privacy and Protection

* Definition: The enterprise ensures the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of customer data.
* Procedure:
	+ Implement encryption for sensitive data in transit and at rest
	+ Conduct regular vulnerability assessments and penetration testing
	+ Develop a incident response plan for data breaches

#### Policy 3: Whistleblower Protection

* Definition: The enterprise protects employees and stakeholders from retaliation for reporting suspected violations of laws, regulations, or internal policies.
* Procedure:
	+ Establish an anonymous reporting mechanism
	+ Conduct regular training on whistleblower protection and reporting procedures
	+ Ensure confidentiality and anonymity for whistleblowers

### 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

#### Monitoring and Reporting

* Schedule: Quarterly compliance monitoring reports to the board of directors
* Criteria:
	+ Adherence to regulatory requirements
	+ Effectiveness of internal controls
	+ Compliance training and awareness programs

#### Penalties and Disciplinary Actions

* Non-compliance with policies or procedures: Verbal warning, written warning, or suspension without pay
* Repeated non-compliance: Termination of employment
* Falsification of records or statements: Immediate termination

#### Operational Governance

* Compliance Committee:
	+ Chairperson: Chief Compliance Officer (CCO)
	+ Members: Designated compliance officers and legal counsel
	+ Purpose: Oversee compliance program, review reports, and make recommendations to the board of directors
* Auditing and Monitoring:
	+ Conduct regular internal audits and risk assessments
	+ Monitor compliance performance metrics and adjust policies as needed
