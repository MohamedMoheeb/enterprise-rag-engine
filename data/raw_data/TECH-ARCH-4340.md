## 1. Document Scope and Executive Summary
This reference document outlines the core policies, standard operating procedures (SOPs), and operational governance for the Architecture department. The purpose of this document is to provide a comprehensive guide for all stakeholders, ensuring consistency in architectural design, development, and deployment across the organization.

### Scope

This document applies to all employees, contractors, and partners involved in architecture-related activities within the organization. It covers the following key areas:

* Architectural design principles
* Software development methodologies
* Technology stack and infrastructure requirements
* Change management procedures
* Compliance monitoring and reporting

### Executive Summary

The Architecture department is responsible for designing, developing, and deploying technology solutions that meet business objectives. This document outlines the policies, procedures, and governance framework necessary to ensure the successful delivery of architectural projects.

## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Architectural Design Principles

* Principle 1: Alignment with Business Objectives
	+ All architecture designs must align with the organization's strategic objectives.
* Principle 2: Modularity and Scalability
	+ Designs should prioritize modularity, scalability, and maintainability.
* Principle 3: Security and Compliance
	+ Designs must adhere to organizational security policies and compliance requirements.

### 2.2 Software Development Methodologies

* Agile Development Process:
	+ Scrum framework will be used for all software development projects.
	+ Iterations will be no longer than 4 weeks, with a maximum of 3 iterations per sprint.
* Waterfall Development Process:
	+ Used for non-Agile projects or when specific requirements dictate.

### 2.3 Technology Stack and Infrastructure Requirements

* Approved Technologies:
	+ List of approved technologies, frameworks, and tools for use in architecture designs.
* Infrastructure Requirements:
	+ Definition of infrastructure requirements for each project, including network, storage, and compute resources.

### 2.4 Change Management Procedures

* Change Request Process:
	+ Requests for changes to architectural designs must be submitted through the change request system.
	+ Changes will be reviewed and approved/disapproved based on business objectives and impact analysis.
* Deployment and Rollback Procedures:
	+ Standard procedures for deploying and rolling back changes to ensure minimal disruption.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

* Regular Audits:
	+ Quarterly audits will be conducted to ensure compliance with architectural design principles, software development methodologies, and technology stack requirements.
* Incident Reporting:
	+ All incidents or non-compliance issues must be reported promptly through the incident reporting system.

### 3.2 Penalties and Consequences

* Non-Compliance Penalties:
	+ First-time offenders: verbal warning
	+ Repeat offenders: written warning
	+ Chronic offenders: disciplinary action, up to termination of employment or contract
* Compliance Incentives:
	+ Recognition awards for outstanding compliance performance
	+ Bonuses for successful project delivery and adherence to architectural design principles

### 3.3 Operational Governance

* Architecture Review Board (ARB):
	+ A governing body responsible for reviewing and approving architectural designs, ensuring alignment with business objectives.
* Architecture Design Committee (ADC):
	+ A committee responsible for developing and maintaining architecture design principles, software development methodologies, and technology stack requirements.

This document serves as a comprehensive guide for the Architecture department. All stakeholders are expected to adhere to the policies, procedures, and governance framework outlined in this document. Failure to comply may result in penalties or consequences as described above.
