## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Employee Classification and Job Descriptions

* All employees will be classified into one of the following categories: full-time, part-time, or temporary.
* Each employee's job description will be updated annually to reflect changes in job responsibilities and expectations.
* The HR department will maintain a centralized database of all employee job descriptions.

### 2.2 Recruitment and Hiring

* All job openings will be posted on the company's internal job board and external job boards (e.g., LinkedIn, Indeed).
* The hiring process will include multiple interviews with the hiring manager and at least one other HR representative.
* New hires will undergo a comprehensive orientation program, covering company policies, benefits, and expectations.

### 2.3 Performance Management

* Each employee will have an annual performance evaluation, conducted by their supervisor or department head.
* Performance evaluations will be based on job descriptions, key performance indicators (KPIs), and company goals.
* Employees will receive written feedback and a rating (1-5) based on their performance.

### 2.4 Employee Development and Training

* The HR department will maintain a centralized database of employee training records.
* All employees will participate in at least one training or development program per year, as required by their job description or company goals.
* Employees will receive written feedback and a rating (1-5) based on the effectiveness of their training.

### 2.5 Leave Policies

* [Company Name] offers a comprehensive leave policy, including paid time off, sick leave, and family and medical leave.
* All employees are required to follow company leave policies and procedures.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

* The HR department will conduct regular compliance monitoring reviews to ensure adherence to company policies and procedures.
* Non-compliance with HR policies or procedures may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment.

### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

* First offense: Verbal warning
* Second offense: Written warning
* Third offense: Suspension (up to one week)
* Fourth offense: Termination of employment

### 3.3 Operational Governance

* The HR department will maintain a centralized database of all employee records and HR-related information.
* All HR-related decisions and actions will be documented and maintained in the central database.
