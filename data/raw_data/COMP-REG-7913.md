## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures
--------------------------------------------------------

### 2.1 Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Policy

The AML policy outlines the measures to prevent and detect money laundering, terrorist financing, and other financial crimes within our organization.

* **Scope:** All employees involved in transactions, customer due diligence, and risk assessment
* **Procedure:**
	+ Conduct thorough customer due diligence for all customers
	+ Monitor and report suspicious transactions
	+ Maintain accurate records of transactions and customer information

### 2.2 Know Your Customer (KYC) Policy

The KYC policy ensures that we have a comprehensive understanding of our customers' identities, risk profiles, and business activities.

* **Scope:** All employees involved in customer onboarding, account management, and transaction monitoring
* **Procedure:**
	+ Verify customer identity through documentary evidence and other means
	+ Assess customer risk profile using standardized criteria
	+ Maintain accurate records of customer information

### 2.3 Data Privacy Policy

The data privacy policy outlines the measures to protect personal and sensitive information within our organization.

* **Scope:** All employees handling or processing personal and sensitive data
* **Procedure:**
	+ Implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to secure data
	+ Conduct regular data audits and risk assessments
	+ Provide training on data protection best practices

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance
-------------------------------------------------------------------

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

The Compliance department will conduct regular monitoring and reporting on the effectiveness of our compliance program.

* **Scope:** All employees involved in compliance-related activities
* **Procedure:**
	+ Conduct regular audits and risk assessments
	+ Review and analyze compliance metrics and trends
	+ Provide training and guidance to employees on compliance best practices

### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

The organization will impose penalties for non-compliance with our policies, SOPs, and regulatory requirements.

* **Scope:** All employees involved in compliance-related activities
* **Procedure:**
	+ Identify and document instances of non-compliance
	+ Impose appropriate disciplinary actions (e.g., warnings, suspensions)
	+ Develop corrective action plans to prevent future incidents

### 3.3 Operational Governance

The Compliance department will work closely with other departments to ensure effective operational governance.

* **Scope:** All employees involved in compliance-related activities
* **Procedure:**
	+ Establish clear roles and responsibilities for compliance-related tasks
	+ Conduct regular training and awareness programs
	+ Foster a culture of compliance and ethical behavior within the organization
