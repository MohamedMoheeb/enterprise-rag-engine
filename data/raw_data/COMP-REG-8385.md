## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1. General Principles

The Compliance department shall operate in accordance with the following general principles:

* Integrity: The department shall maintain the highest level of integrity and professionalism in its operations.
* Transparency: The department shall ensure that all compliance-related activities are transparent, accurate, and thoroughly documented.
* Accountability: The department shall be accountable for its actions and decisions.

### 2.2. Compliance Framework

The Compliance department shall adhere to a comprehensive framework consisting of the following components:

* Policy Development: The department shall develop and maintain policies that align with relevant laws, regulations, and industry standards.
* Procedure Documentation: The department shall document standard operating procedures for compliance-related activities.
* Training and Awareness: The department shall provide regular training and awareness programs for employees on compliance matters.

### 2.3. Core Policies

The following core policies shall govern the Compliance department's activities:

* Data Protection Policy
* Anti-Corruption Policy
* Whistleblower Policy
* Code of Conduct Policy

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1. Compliance Monitoring

The Compliance department shall conduct regular monitoring activities to ensure compliance with relevant laws, regulations, and industry standards. This may include:

* Audits: The department shall conduct periodic audits to assess compliance with policies and procedures.
* Risk Assessments: The department shall identify and assess potential risks and develop mitigation strategies.

### 3.2. Penalties

The Compliance department shall impose penalties for non-compliance with relevant laws, regulations, and industry standards. These penalties may include:

* Disciplinary Action: The department may take disciplinary action against employees who fail to comply with policies and procedures.
* Financial Penalties: The department may impose financial penalties on individuals or entities that violate compliance requirements.

### 3.3. Operational Governance

The Compliance department shall operate in accordance with the following governance framework:

* Reporting: The department shall report compliance-related issues and findings to senior management and the board of directors.
* Escalation Procedures: The department shall establish escalation procedures for addressing compliance-related incidents.
* Continuous Improvement: The department shall continuously review and improve its operations to ensure effectiveness and efficiency.
