## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures


### 2.1 Architecture Design Principles


The Architecture department shall adhere to the following design principles:

* Scalability: Designs must be scalable to accommodate future growth and changes
* Security: All designs must prioritize security and minimize vulnerabilities
* Usability: User-centered design approaches shall be employed to ensure intuitive user experiences
* Performance: Designs shall optimize performance, minimizing latency and improving responsiveness

### 2.2 Architecture Development Process


The Architecture department shall follow the standard development process outlined below:

1. Requirements Gathering: Gather and document requirements from stakeholders
2. Design Phase: Develop architecture design based on gathered requirements
3. Review and Revision: Conduct reviews and revisions to ensure design meets requirements and standards
4. Implementation: Implement designed architecture, ensuring compliance with relevant regulations and industry standards

### 2.3 Architecture Deployment and Maintenance


The Architecture department shall follow the standard deployment and maintenance process outlined below:

1. Deployment: Deploy implemented architecture, ensuring minimal disruption to existing systems
2. Monitoring: Monitor deployed architecture for performance, security, and usability issues
3. Maintenance: Perform regular maintenance tasks to ensure continued optimal performance and minimize downtime

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance


### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring


The Architecture department shall conduct regular compliance monitoring activities to ensure adherence to:

* Industry standards (e.g., SOA, TOGAF)
* Relevant regulations (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA)
* Company policies and procedures

### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance


In the event of non-compliance with policies, SOPs, or regulatory requirements, the Architecture department shall:

* Document incidents and root causes
* Implement corrective actions to prevent reoccurrence
* Report incidents to management and relevant stakeholders

### 3.3 Operational Governance


The Architecture department shall adhere to the following operational governance principles:

* Transparency: Maintain transparency in architecture design, development, and deployment processes
* Accountability: Ensure accountability for architecture-related activities and decisions
* Continuous Improvement: Foster a culture of continuous improvement, encouraging learning from mistakes and best practices
