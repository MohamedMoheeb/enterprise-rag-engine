# TECH-ARCH-2338: Architecture Reference Document
## Department: Architecture
## Author: Joseph Poole
## Effective Date: 2024-06-06

# 1. Document Scope and Executive Summary

This Architecture Reference Document (ARD) provides a comprehensive guide to the Architecture department's policies, procedures, and guidelines for designing and implementing enterprise-level architecture solutions. The document outlines the department's scope, roles, and responsibilities, as well as the standards and best practices for architectural design, development, and deployment.

The ARD is intended to serve as a reference guide for architects, engineers, developers, and other stakeholders involved in the development of software applications, systems, and infrastructure. It provides a framework for designing and implementing architecture solutions that meet the organization's business requirements and technical standards.

# 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### Policy: Architectural Governance

The Architecture department is responsible for ensuring that all architectural designs and implementations align with the organization's overall strategy and goals. The department will adhere to the following principles:

* All architecture decisions will be based on a thorough understanding of the business requirements and technical constraints.
* Architectures will be designed to be flexible, scalable, and maintainable.
* Changes to architectures will be managed through a controlled process.

### Procedure: Architecture Design and Review

1. The Architecture department will receive requests for architecture design from various stakeholders.
2. The department will conduct a thorough analysis of the business requirements and technical constraints to develop an architecture design.
3. The design will be reviewed by a team of architects and engineers to ensure that it meets the organization's standards and guidelines.
4. The final design will be presented to the relevant stakeholders for approval.

### Procedure: Architecture Development and Deployment

1. The Architecture department will work with development teams to develop architecture solutions based on the approved design.
2. The department will provide technical guidance and support during the development process.
3. The deployed architecture solution will be tested and validated against the business requirements and technical standards.

# 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### Compliance Monitoring

The Architecture department will monitor compliance with the policies and procedures outlined in this document. This includes:

* Conducting regular audits to ensure that architectural designs and implementations align with the organization's strategy and goals.
* Providing training and guidance to architects, engineers, and developers on the department's policies and procedures.

### Penalties for Non-Compliance

 Failure to comply with the policies and procedures outlined in this document may result in penalties, including:

* Warning or reprimand
* Suspension of architecture design and development activities
* Termination of employment

### Operational Governance

The Architecture department will operate under the following principles:

* The department will maintain a comprehensive and up-to-date inventory of all architecture solutions.
* The department will provide technical guidance and support to developers, engineers, and other stakeholders involved in the development and deployment of architecture solutions.
* The department will collaborate with other departments and teams to ensure that architecture solutions align with business requirements and technical standards.
