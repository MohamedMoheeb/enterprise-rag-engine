# 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Architectural Standards

The Architecture department adheres to the following architectural standards:

* [Standard 1]: Define and document all architectural designs, specifications, and requirements.

### 2.2 Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

The Architecture department follows the following SOPs:

* **Design and Development**: Define and document architectural designs and specifications; develop and test architectural solutions; implement and deploy architectural solutions.
* **Change Management**: Identify, assess, and prioritize architectural changes; develop and implement change management plans; track and report on change outcomes.
* **Documentation and Record-Keeping**: Maintain accurate and up-to-date records of all architectural activities, including design decisions, implementation details, and lessons learned.

# 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

The Architecture department conducts regular compliance monitoring to ensure adherence to architectural standards, SOPs, and governance framework. This includes:

* Regular audits of architectural designs, specifications, and changes
* Review of architecture-related documentation and records
* Assessment of architecture-related risks and opportunities

### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

The Architecture department will impose penalties on individuals or teams that fail to comply with architectural standards, SOPs, and governance framework. The penalties may include:

* Written warnings or reprimands
* Suspension or termination of architectural activities
* Mandatory training or education programs
* Financial penalties or fines

### 3.3 Operational Governance

The Architecture department operates under the following governance framework:

* **Architecture Charter**: Define the company's architecture vision, goals, and objectives.
* **Architecture Roadmap**: Develop a long-term architecture roadmap to align with business strategies and priorities.
* **Architecture Governance Board**: Establish an oversight body to ensure alignment between architectural solutions and business requirements.

By adhering to this reference document, the Architecture department will ensure that all architectural activities are conducted in accordance with company standards, policies, and governance framework.
