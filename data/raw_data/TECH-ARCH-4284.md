# TECH-ARCH-4284: Architecture Reference Document

Document ID: TECH-ARCH-4284
Department: Architecture
Author: Paula Cooper
Effective Date: 2026-04-16

## 1. Document Scope and Executive Summary

This document outlines the Architecture department's policies, procedures, and guidelines for ensuring the effective design, development, and implementation of architectural solutions within the organization. The scope of this document includes all architecture-related activities, including system architecture, infrastructure planning, data modeling, and software design.

The primary objective of this document is to provide a comprehensive framework for architects to follow when designing and implementing architectural solutions that meet the organization's technical, business, and security requirements.

## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Architecture Design Principles

* All architecture designs must adhere to the organization's strategic goals and objectives.
* Designs must be scalable, flexible, and adaptable to changing business needs.
* Security, reliability, and performance are essential considerations in all design decisions.

### 2.2 Design Reviews and Approvals

* All architectural designs must undergo a formal review process before implementation.
* The review process will ensure that designs meet the organization's technical, business, and security requirements.
* Approval authority for architecture designs lies with the Architecture Director or their designated representative.

### 2.3 Change Management

* Changes to existing architectures must be documented and approved through the change management process.
* All changes must be assessed for impact on system performance, security, and compliance.

### 2.4 Code Quality and Standards

* All code must adhere to established coding standards and guidelines.
* Code reviews will be conducted regularly to ensure adherence to these standards.

### 2.5 Infrastructure Planning and Management

* The Architecture department is responsible for planning and managing the organization's infrastructure needs.
* This includes ensuring adequate capacity, security, and availability of all systems and networks.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

* The Architecture department will conduct regular compliance monitoring to ensure adherence to established policies, procedures, and standards.
* Compliance monitoring will include reviews of architecture designs, code quality, and infrastructure planning.

### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

* Failure to comply with established policies, procedures, and standards may result in penalties, including but not limited to:
	+ Reprimand
	+ Training or education requirements
	+ Suspension or termination of architecture design authority

### 3.3 Operational Governance

* The Architecture department will maintain a governance framework for managing architectural solutions.
* This framework will include roles and responsibilities, decision-making processes, and communication protocols.
