## 1. Document Scope and Executive Summary

This reference document outlines the core policies, standard operating procedures (SOPs), and guidelines for the Architecture department at [Company Name]. The purpose of this document is to provide a comprehensive framework for architects, designers, and stakeholders to ensure the delivery of high-quality architectural solutions that meet business objectives.

The scope of this document includes:

* Defining the role and responsibilities of the Architecture department
* Outlining the core policies and SOPs for architecture development
* Describing compliance monitoring, penalties, and operational governance

## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Design Principles

* Adhere to [Company Name]'s brand guidelines for visual identity and user experience
* Follow the [Company Name] design system for consistency across products and services
* Ensure designs are accessible, intuitive, and visually appealing

### 2.2 Architecture Development Process

* Initiate architecture development projects through the [Company Name] project management tool
* Collaborate with stakeholders to define project scope, goals, and timelines
* Develop detailed architecture designs, including system architecture diagrams, component descriptions, and technical requirements
* Conduct peer reviews and iterative design refinement to ensure high-quality solutions

### 2.3 Architecture Review and Approval Process

* Submit architecture designs for review and approval through the [Company Name] architecture review process
* Obtain feedback from peers and stakeholders on design effectiveness, feasibility, and impact
* Address reviewer comments and iterate on designs until approved

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

* Regularly review architecture designs for adherence to [Company Name] policies, standards, and guidelines
* Conduct quarterly audits to ensure compliance with architecture development process and design principles

### 3.2 Penalties

* Failure to comply with architecture development process and design principles may result in:
	+ Revisions or rework of architecture designs
	+ Delayed project timelines
	+ Escalation to management for further guidance or disciplinary action

### 3.3 Operational Governance

* Establish and maintain a centralized architecture repository for storing and sharing architecture designs
* Develop and maintain architecture documentation, including design rationales, technical requirements, and implementation plans
* Collaborate with other departments (e.g., engineering, product management) to ensure aligned architecture strategies
