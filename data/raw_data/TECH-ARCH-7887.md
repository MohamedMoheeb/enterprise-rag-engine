# TECH-ARCH-7887: Architecture Reference Document

## Department: Architecture
## Author: Jeffrey Williams
## Effective Date: 2024-01-19

### Executive Summary:

This reference document outlines the core policies, standard operating procedures (SOPs), and governance guidelines for the Architecture department. The purpose of this document is to provide a comprehensive framework for designing, developing, and maintaining our company's architectural assets.

## 1. Document Scope and Executive Summary

### Purpose:

The Architecture Reference Document serves as a central resource for all stakeholders involved in architecture-related activities within the organization. This document aims to establish a shared understanding of our architectural principles, standards, and best practices, ensuring consistency and efficiency across all projects and teams.

### Scope:

This document applies to all employees, contractors, and partners involved in architecture-related activities, including:

* Architects
* Engineers
* Designers
* Project Managers
* Stakeholders

### Definitions:

For the purpose of this document, the following definitions apply:

* Architecture: The process of designing and developing our company's digital products, systems, and services.
* Architectural Assets: All design-related deliverables, including but not limited to:
	+ Design concepts and prototypes
	+ Technical documentation (e.g., architecture diagrams, flowcharts)
	+ Code and software development

## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### Policy 1: Architecture Governance

The Architecture department is responsible for governing all architecture-related activities within the organization. This includes:

* Defining and maintaining architectural standards
* Reviewing and approving design concepts and prototypes
* Ensuring compliance with organizational policies and industry best practices

### Procedure 1: Design Concept Development

1. Initial Design Concepts:
	+ Identify project requirements and stakeholders' needs.
	+ Develop initial design concepts, including sketches, wireframes, or prototypes.
2. Design Review and Approval:
	+ Submit design concepts for review and approval by the Architecture department.
	+ Address any feedback or concerns raised during the review process.

### Procedure 2: Technical Documentation

1. Create Technical Documentation:
	+ Prepare technical documentation (e.g., architecture diagrams, flowcharts) to support design decisions and implementation.
	+ Ensure documentation is accurate, complete, and easily accessible.
2. Update and Maintain:
	+ Regularly update and maintain technical documentation as designs evolve or change.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### Compliance Monitoring:

The Architecture department will monitor compliance with this document's policies and procedures through regular audits, reviews, and spot checks.

### Non-Compliance Penalties:

In the event of non-compliance, the following penalties may apply:

* First offense: Warning and re-training
* Second offense: Suspension of architecture-related activities for a specified period
* Third offense: Termination of employment or contract

### Operational Governance:

The Architecture department will maintain records of all architecture-related activities, including design concepts, prototypes, and technical documentation. This information will be used to track progress, identify areas for improvement, and ensure compliance with organizational policies and industry best practices.
