# TECH-ARCH-1788: Architecture Reference Document
## Department: Architecture
## Author: Edward Collins
## Effective Date: 2021-04-05

# 1. Document Scope and Executive Summary
This reference document outlines the core policies, standard operating procedures, and guidelines for the Architecture department at [Company Name]. The purpose of this document is to provide a comprehensive framework for architects, engineers, and other stakeholders to ensure consistency, efficiency, and compliance with industry standards and company regulations.

The scope of this document covers all aspects of architecture, including design, development, testing, deployment, and maintenance of software applications, systems, and infrastructure. This reference document applies to all employees involved in the Architecture department, as well as external partners and vendors working on behalf of the company.

# 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1. Design Principles
Architects shall adhere to the following design principles:

* Separation of Concerns (SoC)
* Single Responsibility Principle (SRP)
* Don't Repeat Yourself (DRY)
* Keep It Simple, Stupid (KISS)

### 2.2. Development Procedures

* All code changes must be reviewed and approved by a peer or supervisor before deployment.
* Code reviews shall include analysis of:
	+ Syntax and formatting
	+ Logical flow and control structures
	+ Error handling and debugging
	+ Code duplication and repetition
* Source control systems (SCMs) shall be used to manage and track code changes.

### 2.3. Testing and Quality Assurance

* A testing framework shall be established for each project, including:
	+ Unit testing
	+ Integration testing
	+ System testing
	+ Acceptance testing
* Test cases shall be documented and maintained throughout the development process.
* All code changes must pass automated tests before deployment.

### 2.4. Deployment and Maintenance

* A deployment framework shall be established for each project, including:
	+ Continuous Integration (CI)
	+ Continuous Delivery (CD)
	+ Continuous Monitoring (CM)
* Deployed systems shall be monitored and maintained regularly to ensure stability and performance.
* Bug fixes and new features shall be prioritized based on business requirements and user feedback.

### 2.5. Communication and Collaboration

* Architects shall collaborate with cross-functional teams, including:
	+ Development
	+ Quality Assurance (QA)
	+ Operations (Ops)
	+ Business Analysis (BA)
* Clear and concise documentation shall be maintained throughout the development process.
* Regular meetings and status updates shall be held to ensure alignment and progress.

# 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1. Compliance Monitoring
The Architecture department shall monitor compliance with industry standards, regulations, and company policies through regular audits and inspections.

### 3.2. Penalties
Failure to comply with architecture guidelines may result in penalties, including but not limited to:

* Delayed project delivery or milestones
* Reduced bonuses or performance ratings
* Required rework or revisions
* Additional training or education

### 3.3. Operational Governance
The Architecture department shall be responsible for the governance and oversight of all architectural decisions and implementations.

* Architects shall maintain accurate and up-to-date documentation of architecture decisions.
* Regular reviews and assessments shall be conducted to ensure compliance with company policies and industry standards.

By following this reference document, the Architecture department at [Company Name] shall ensure the delivery of high-quality software applications, systems, and infrastructure that meet business requirements and user needs.
