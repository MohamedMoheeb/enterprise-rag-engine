# TECH-ARCH-2763: Architecture Reference Document

## Department: Architecture
## Author: Holly Park
## Effective Date: 2025-01-16

### 1. Document Scope and Executive Summary

This reference document outlines the architecture department's policies, procedures, and guidelines for designing, developing, and maintaining the organization's software systems, infrastructure, and technology assets. The purpose of this document is to provide a comprehensive framework for ensuring the quality, security, and scalability of our technology solutions.

The scope of this document includes:

* Software development methodologies
* Technology stack and infrastructure standards
* Security and compliance frameworks
* Collaboration and communication protocols
* Change management processes

### 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

#### 2.1 Software Development Methodologies

The architecture department shall adhere to the following software development methodologies:

* Agile: Scrum framework for iterative development
* Waterfall: Linear approach for sequential development
* Hybrid: Combination of Agile and Waterfall approaches

#### 2.2 Technology Stack and Infrastructure Standards

The architecture department shall maintain a standardized technology stack and infrastructure, including:

* Programming languages: Java, Python, C#
* Databases: MySQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB
* Operating Systems: Windows, Linux, macOS
* Cloud Services: AWS, Azure, Google Cloud

#### 2.3 Security and Compliance Frameworks

The architecture department shall adhere to the following security and compliance frameworks:

* ISO 27001: Information Security Management System
* HIPAA: Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act
* GDPR: General Data Protection Regulation

### 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

#### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

The architecture department shall conduct regular audits and reviews to ensure compliance with the policies and procedures outlined in this document.

#### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

 Failure to comply with the policies and procedures outlined in this document may result in:

* Verbal warnings
* Written warnings
* Suspension or termination of access to technology assets
* Disciplinary action up to and including termination

#### 3.3 Operational Governance

The architecture department shall maintain a governance structure, including:

* Architecture Review Board (ARB): Responsible for reviewing and approving architectural designs
* Technical Leadership Team: Provides technical guidance and oversight
* Change Management Committee: Manages changes to technology assets and infrastructure
