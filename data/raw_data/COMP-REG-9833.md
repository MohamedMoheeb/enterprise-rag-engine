The Compliance Monitoring Framework outlines the frequency, scope, and methodology for conducting compliance monitoring activities. This framework ensures that all areas of the organization are regularly assessed for compliance with relevant policies, regulations, and industry best practices.

* First-time violators: Verbal warning
* Repeat offenders: Written warning
* Serious violations: Disciplinary action (up to and including termination)
