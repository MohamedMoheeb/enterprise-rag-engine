* Outlining the standard operating procedures for architecture-related activities
* Describing the compliance monitoring process and associated penalties

### 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

#### 2.1 Architecture Principles

The following architecture principles will guide the design, development, and implementation of architectural solutions:

* Separation of Concerns (SoC): Each layer or component should have a single, well-defined responsibility.
* Abstraction: Higher-level abstractions should be built on top of lower-level abstractions to reduce complexity.
* Modularity: Components should be modular, reusable, and easy to maintain.
* Scalability: Architectural solutions should be designed to scale horizontally and vertically as needed.

#### 2.2 Design Guidelines

The following design guidelines will apply to all architecture-related activities:

* Use a consistent naming convention for components, layers, and interfaces.
* Ensure that each component has a clear and well-defined interface.
* Use a version control system to track changes and maintain a record of all architectural decisions.
* Document all architectural designs, including assumptions, constraints, and trade-offs.

#### 2.3 Standard Operating Procedures

The following standard operating procedures will be followed for architecture-related activities:

* Architecture Review Board (ARB) Meetings: The ARB will meet bi-weekly to review and approve architectural designs.
* Change Management Process: Changes to architectural designs will be subject to a change management process, which includes approval from the ARB.
* Code Reviews: All code changes will be reviewed by at least two architects before being approved for deployment.

### 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

#### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

The Architecture department will monitor compliance with architectural principles, design guidelines, and standard operating procedures through:

* Regular ARB meetings to review and approve architectural designs.
* Code reviews to ensure adherence to coding standards.
* Quarterly architecture compliance reports to the management team.

#### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

Penalties for non-compliance with architectural principles, design guidelines, and standard operating procedures will include:

* Warning: The first instance of non-compliance will result in a warning from the ARB.
* Fine: Subsequent instances of non-compliance will result in a fine, which will be deducted from the architect's salary.
* Suspension: Repeat offenders may be suspended from architecture-related activities for a specified period.

#### 3.3 Operational Governance

The Architecture department will operate under the following governance structure:

* The Chief Architect will oversee the Architecture department and ensure compliance with architectural principles, design guidelines, and standard operating procedures.
* The ARB will review and approve architectural designs, as well as make decisions on architecture-related matters.
* The Architecture department will maintain a centralized repository of all architectural designs, including assumptions, constraints, and trade-offs.
