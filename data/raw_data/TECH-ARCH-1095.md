# Architecture Reference Document: TECH-ARCH-1095
## 1. Document Scope and Executive Summary


This reference document outlines the core policies, standard operating procedures (SOPs), and governance framework for the Architecture department at [Company Name]. The purpose of this document is to provide a comprehensive guide for architects, project managers, and stakeholders on how to effectively design, develop, and deliver high-quality architecture solutions that align with the company's overall strategy.


## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures


### 2.1 Architecture Principles


The following principles will guide our architectural decisions:

* Alignment: Ensure that all architectures are aligned with the company's overall strategy.
* Flexibility: Design for flexibility to accommodate future changes and adaptability.
* Scalability: Develop architectures that can scale horizontally and vertically as needed.
* Security: Prioritize security and ensure that all architectures meet or exceed industry standards.
* Interoperability: Foster interoperability across different systems, services, and applications.


### 2.2 Architecture Development Process


The architecture development process will follow these stages:

1. Problem Definition: Identify the problem or opportunity and define the scope of the project.
2. Research and Analysis: Conduct research and analysis to understand the requirements and constraints.
3. Design and Prototyping: Develop a design and prototype based on the findings.
4. Review and Revision: Obtain feedback, revise as needed, and finalize the architecture.
5. Implementation and Deployment: Implement and deploy the architecture.

### 2.3 Change Management


The following change management process will be followed:

1. Request for Change (RFC): Submit a formal request for a change to the architecture.
2. Review and Evaluation: Evaluate the impact of the proposed change on the overall system.
3. Approval or Rejection: Approve or reject the change based on its alignment with the company's strategy and the potential risks.

### 2.4 Architecture Governance


The following governance framework will be established:

1. Architecture Steering Committee (ASC): A committee composed of stakeholders responsible for overseeing the architecture development process.
2. Architecture Review Board (ARB): A board responsible for reviewing and approving architectures.
3. Architecture Champions: Subject matter experts who champion specific architectural areas.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance


### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring


The following compliance monitoring processes will be implemented:

1. Regular Audits: Conduct regular audits to ensure adherence to the architecture principles and guidelines.
2. Incident Response: Respond promptly to any incidents or issues that may arise.

### 3.2 Penalties


In case of non-compliance, the following penalties will apply:

* Minor Non-Compliance: Verbal warning
* Moderate Non-Compliance: Written warning and mandatory training
* Major Non-Compliance: Suspension or termination of access to architecture resources

### 3.3 Operational Governance


The following operational governance framework will be established:

1. Architecture Roadmap: Develop a roadmap outlining the company's architectural priorities.
2. Resource Allocation: Allocate resources as needed to support the architecture development process.
3. Communication Plan: Establish a communication plan for stakeholders and team members.

By adhering to this reference document, we can ensure that our architectures are well-designed, scalable, secure, and aligned with the company's overall strategy.
