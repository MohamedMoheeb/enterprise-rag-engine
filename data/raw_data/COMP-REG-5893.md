# COMP-REG-5893: Compliance Reference Document
## Department: Compliance
## Author: Sean Pacheco
## Effective Date: 2020-03-16

### Document Scope and Executive Summary

This reference document outlines the core policies, standard operating procedures (SOPs), and operational governance for the Compliance department. The purpose of this document is to provide a comprehensive framework for ensuring compliance with relevant laws, regulations, and industry standards.

The scope of this document includes:

* Overview of the Compliance department's role in ensuring organizational compliance
* Core policies governing business practices and operations
* Standard operating procedures (SOPs) for conducting audits, investigations, and monitoring activities
* Guidelines for handling non-compliance incidents and reporting requirements

### 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

#### 2.1 Policy: Compliance Framework

The Compliance department is responsible for developing, implementing, and maintaining a comprehensive compliance framework that ensures the organization's business practices and operations are in line with relevant laws, regulations, and industry standards.

#### SOP: Conducting Audits and Investigations

* Purpose: To ensure compliance with regulatory requirements and organizational policies
* Scope: All areas of the organization's operations, including but not limited to finance, human resources, procurement, and sales
* Procedures:
	1. Identify audit or investigation scope and objectives
	2. Develop audit or investigation plan and schedule
	3. Conduct on-site audits or investigations as necessary
	4. Document findings and recommendations
	5. Present findings and recommendations to management for corrective action

#### SOP: Monitoring and Reporting

* Purpose: To ensure ongoing compliance with regulatory requirements and organizational policies
* Scope: All areas of the organization's operations, including but not limited to finance, human resources, procurement, and sales
* Procedures:
	1. Identify areas requiring monitoring and reporting
	2. Develop monitoring and reporting plan and schedule
	3. Conduct regular monitoring and reporting activities
	4. Document findings and recommendations
	5. Present findings and recommendations to management for corrective action

### 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

#### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

The Compliance department is responsible for conducting ongoing compliance monitoring activities to ensure the organization's business practices and operations are in line with relevant laws, regulations, and industry standards.

#### 3.2 Penalties and Sanctions

In the event of non-compliance, the following penalties and sanctions may be imposed:

* Written warning
* Suspension or termination of employment
* Financial penalties or fines
* Loss of certifications or licenses

#### 3.3 Operational Governance

The Compliance department is responsible for ensuring that all areas of the organization's operations are governed by policies, procedures, and standards that promote compliance with relevant laws, regulations, and industry standards.

This document outlines the core policies, SOPs, and operational governance for the Compliance department. It is intended to provide a comprehensive framework for ensuring compliance with relevant laws, regulations, and industry standards.
