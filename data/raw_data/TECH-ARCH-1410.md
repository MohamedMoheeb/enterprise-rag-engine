## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Architecture Principles

The Architecture department follows the following core principles:

* **Modularity**: Designs shall be modular, scalable, and maintainable.
* **Reusability**: Components shall be designed for reuse across multiple projects.
* **Flexibility**: Architectures shall be adaptable to changing business needs.
* **Security**: Designs shall prioritize security and minimize vulnerabilities.

### 2.2 Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

1. **Design Reviews**:
	* Conduct regular design reviews with stakeholders and team members.
	* Ensure designs meet departmental standards and are aligned with company goals.
2. **Code Quality Control**:
	* Enforce coding standards, best practices, and guidelines.
	* Conduct code quality control checks for all submitted code.
3. **Change Management**:
	* Implement a change management process for architecture changes.
	* Ensure that all stakeholders are informed of architectural changes and their impact.

### 2.3 Architecture Standards

1. **Technology Stack**: Utilize [Company Name]'s approved technology stack.
2. **Database Standards**: Follow [Company Name]'s database standards and best practices.
3. **Security Guidelines**: Adhere to [Company Name]'s security guidelines and standards.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

The Architecture department shall monitor compliance with this document through:

* Regular design reviews
* Code quality control checks
* Change management process tracking

### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

Non-compliance with this document may result in:

* Verbal warnings
* Written warnings
* Suspension or termination of employment

### 3.3 Operational Governance

The Architecture department shall maintain a governance structure to ensure the effective operation of the department, including:

* **Architecture Review Board**: A committee responsible for reviewing and approving architectural designs.
* **Architecture Working Group**: A group responsible for discussing architecture-related topics and providing guidance.

By following this document, the Architecture department will maintain consistency, efficiency, and effectiveness in its operations. Any questions or concerns regarding this document should be directed to the Architecture department leadership.
