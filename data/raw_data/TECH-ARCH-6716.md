## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Architecture Development Process

The Architecture department follows a structured approach to design and develop architectures. The process includes:

1. Requirements gathering: Gather requirements from stakeholders through workshops, interviews, and surveys.
2. Design phase: Develop high-level architecture designs based on gathered requirements.
3. Review and iteration: Conduct peer reviews and iterate on the design until it meets stakeholder expectations.
4. Implementation: Implement the designed architecture using [Company Name]'s standard tools and frameworks.

### 2.2 Architecture Governance

The Architecture department is responsible for ensuring that all architectures are aligned with [Company Name]'s strategic objectives, business goals, and technical standards. This includes:

1. Architecture review committee: Establish a review committee to evaluate and approve architecture designs.
2. Change management: Implement change management processes to ensure that changes to existing architectures are properly assessed and approved.

### 2.3 Communication and Collaboration

The Architecture department is responsible for effective communication and collaboration with other departments, including:

1. Stakeholder engagement: Engage with stakeholders to gather requirements and provide architecture designs.
2. Team collaboration: Collaborate with developers, project managers, and other teams to ensure successful implementation of architectures.

### 2.4 Quality Assurance

The Architecture department ensures that all architecture designs meet [Company Name]'s quality standards by:

1. Conducting peer reviews: Conduct regular peer reviews to ensure that design meets expectations.
2. Testing and validation: Test and validate architecture designs using [Company Name]'s standard testing frameworks.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

The Architecture department is responsible for monitoring compliance with company policies, standards, and regulations. This includes:

1. Regular audits: Conduct regular audits to ensure adherence to architectural guidelines and standards.
2. Reporting: Provide regular reports on compliance status to stakeholders.

### 3.2 Penalties and Corrective Actions

In case of non-compliance or deviations from established procedures, the Architecture department will:

1. Document incidents: Document all incidents and corrective actions taken.
2. Conduct root cause analysis: Analyze the root cause of incidents and implement corrective actions to prevent recurrence.

### 3.3 Operational Governance

The Architecture department is responsible for ensuring that its operations are governed by [Company Name]'s policies, standards, and procedures. This includes:

1. Budgeting: Establish and manage budgets for architecture projects.
2. Resource allocation: Allocate resources (time, personnel, and equipment) to ensure successful project delivery.
