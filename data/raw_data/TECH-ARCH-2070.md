# TECH-ARCH-2070: Architecture Reference Document
## Department: Architecture
## Author: Beth Cross
## Effective Date: 2022-04-05

### Document Scope and Executive Summary

This document outlines the core policies, standard operating procedures (SOPs), and guidelines for the Architecture department. The purpose of this reference document is to provide a comprehensive framework for architects, engineers, and other stakeholders to ensure consistency, efficiency, and compliance with company standards.

The Architecture department is responsible for designing, developing, and maintaining the organization's technology infrastructure, including software applications, data centers, networks, and systems architecture. This document aims to standardize best practices, clarify roles and responsibilities, and promote collaboration among team members.

### 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

#### 2.1 Architecture Governance

* The Architecture department shall be responsible for ensuring that all technology-related projects adhere to the company's overall strategy and architecture principles.
* A dedicated Architecture Governance Committee will oversee the review and approval of architecture designs, ensuring alignment with organizational goals.

#### 2.2 Design Principles

* The Architecture department shall develop and maintain a set of design principles, outlining key considerations for system architecture, software development, and infrastructure planning.
* Design principles shall be reviewed and updated annually to ensure they remain relevant and effective.

#### 2.3 Change Management Process

* The Architecture department shall establish a change management process for all technology-related changes, including updates, patches, and migrations.
* This process shall involve stakeholder notification, impact assessment, and approval before implementation.

### 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

#### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

* The Architecture department shall conduct regular compliance monitoring to ensure adherence to company standards, policies, and procedures.
* A quarterly report will be submitted to the Architecture Governance Committee detailing any non-compliance issues and corrective actions taken.

#### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

* Failure to comply with architecture standards or procedures may result in penalties, including:
	+ Verbal warnings
	+ Written warnings
	+ Suspension of access to company systems or infrastructure
	+ Termination of employment

#### 3.3 Operational Governance

* The Architecture department shall establish a framework for operational governance, outlining roles and responsibilities for system administration, maintenance, and troubleshooting.
* A dedicated Operations Team will be responsible for ensuring the smooth operation of technology infrastructure, including monitoring, reporting, and incident resolution.

By following this reference document, the Architecture department aims to promote consistency, efficiency, and compliance with company standards, ultimately ensuring the successful delivery of technology solutions that meet organizational goals.
