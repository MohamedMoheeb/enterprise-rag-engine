# TECH-ARCH-1331: Architecture Reference Document

Document ID: TECH-ARCH-1331
Department: Architecture
Author: Norma Taylor
Effective Date: 2022-04-07

## 1. Document Scope and Executive Summary

This Architecture Reference Document (ARD) outlines the core policies, standard operating procedures, and governance framework for the Architecture department at [Company Name]. The ARD serves as a comprehensive guide for architects, stakeholders, and management to ensure consistency, quality, and compliance in architectural design and development.

### Purpose and Scope

The purpose of this document is to establish a clear understanding of the architecture team's role, responsibilities, and best practices. This ARD applies to all architecture-related activities, including system design, implementation, testing, and maintenance.

## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Architecture Principles

* [Company Name] adheres to a service-oriented architecture (SOA) approach, emphasizing modular, scalable, and maintainable systems.
* The architecture team prioritizes simplicity, flexibility, and reuse in design decisions.
* All architectural designs must align with the company's overall strategy and goals.

### 2.2 Design Process

1. **Requirements Gathering**: Architects work closely with stakeholders to gather and clarify requirements for new or updated systems.
2. **Design Creation**: Architects develop detailed system designs, including architecture diagrams, technical specifications, and implementation plans.
3. **Review and Approval**: Designs are reviewed and approved by the architecture team lead and/or other relevant stakeholders.

### 2.3 Change Management

* The architecture team maintains a centralized change management process for tracking and implementing design changes.
* Changes must be documented, reviewed, and approved according to the company's standard change management procedures.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

The architecture team is responsible for monitoring compliance with this ARD and reporting any deviations or concerns to management.

### 3.2 Penalties and Consequences

 Failure to comply with the provisions of this ARD may result in:
* Verbal warnings
* Written reprimands
* Mandatory training or education programs
* Performance-based penalties, including salary deductions or demotions

### 3.3 Operational Governance

The architecture team will adhere to the following operational governance practices:

* Regular meetings with stakeholders and management to ensure alignment and progress.
* Maintenance of accurate records and documentation for all architectural designs and decisions.
* Continuous monitoring and improvement of the design process and quality.

By adhering to this Architecture Reference Document, the Architecture department at [Company Name] will maintain its commitment to delivering high-quality, scalable, and maintainable systems that align with the company's overall strategy.
