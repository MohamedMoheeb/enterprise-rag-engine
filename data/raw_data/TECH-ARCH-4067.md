# ARCHITECTURE REFERENCE DOCUMENT: TECH-ARCH-4067
## Department: Architecture
## Author: Tracey Norris
## Effective Date: 2023-08-29

### Document Scope and Executive Summary

This Architecture Reference Document (ARD) outlines the core policies, standard operating procedures, and compliance guidelines for the Architecture department. The purpose of this document is to provide a comprehensive framework for architects, engineers, and other stakeholders to ensure adherence to established best practices, industry standards, and company policies.

The ARD covers key aspects such as design principles, system architecture, data management, security protocols, and collaboration procedures. By following these guidelines, the Architecture department aims to deliver high-quality solutions that meet business objectives while ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements and industry norms.

### 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

#### Design Principles

* The Architecture department shall adhere to the company's overall strategy and vision.
* Designs shall be based on established standards, patterns, and best practices.
* All designs shall be reviewed and approved by relevant stakeholders before implementation.

#### System Architecture

* The Architecture department shall develop system architectures that align with business requirements and industry standards.
* Systems shall be designed for scalability, reliability, and maintainability.
* All system architectures shall be documented and maintained in the company's architecture repository.

#### Data Management

* The Architecture department shall ensure data integrity, confidentiality, and availability.
* Data management practices shall comply with relevant regulations (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA).
* Data storage and processing shall follow established standards and guidelines.

#### Security Protocols

* The Architecture department shall implement security protocols that protect against unauthorized access, tampering, or theft of company assets.
* All systems shall be designed with security in mind, incorporating features such as authentication, authorization, and encryption.
* Regular security audits and risk assessments shall be conducted to identify potential vulnerabilities.

#### Collaboration Procedures

* The Architecture department shall collaborate with cross-functional teams (e.g., development, QA, operations) to ensure effective communication and knowledge sharing.
* All team members shall adhere to established collaboration tools and processes.
* Regular meetings and status updates shall be held to ensure project progress and alignment with business objectives.

### 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

#### Compliance Monitoring

* The Architecture department shall conduct regular compliance monitoring and risk assessments to identify potential non-compliance issues.
* All findings shall be documented and reported to relevant stakeholders for corrective action.

#### Penalties and Remediation

* Non-compliance with established policies or procedures may result in penalties, including but not limited to:
	+ Warning letters
	+ Training and re-certification requirements
	+ Suspension or termination of responsibilities
	+ Disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment
* Remediation plans shall be developed and implemented to address compliance issues.

#### Operational Governance

* The Architecture department shall establish and maintain effective operational governance processes, including:
	+ Regular project meetings and status updates
	+ Change management procedures
	+ Issue tracking and resolution processes
	+ Performance metrics and key performance indicators (KPIs)

By following the guidelines outlined in this ARD, the Architecture department aims to deliver high-quality solutions that meet business objectives while ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements and industry norms.
