## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance
### A. Compliance Monitoring

The Compliance Department is responsible for monitoring compliance with company policies and applicable laws and regulations. This includes:

* Conducting regular audits and risk assessments
* Reviewing compliance metrics and KPIs
* Investigating suspected violations or irregularities
* Implementing corrective actions and remediation plans as needed

### B. Penalties and Disciplinary Actions

The following penalties and disciplinary actions may be imposed for non-compliance with company policies:

* Verbal warnings
* Written warnings
* Suspension without pay
* Termination of employment
* Civil or criminal penalties, as applicable

### C. Operational Governance

To ensure effective compliance operations, the Compliance Department will:

* Establish clear roles and responsibilities
* Develop and maintain SOPs for compliance-related activities
* Provide regular training and awareness programs for employees
* Foster a culture of ethics, integrity, and transparency within the organization
