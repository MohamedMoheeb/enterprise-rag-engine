# COMP-REG-2317: Compliance Reference Document
## Department: Compliance
## Author: Donald Johnson
## Effective Date: 2021-08-13

# 1. Document Scope and Executive Summary
This reference document provides a comprehensive overview of the Compliance department's policies, standard operating procedures, and guidelines for monitoring and addressing compliance issues within [Company Name]. The purpose of this document is to establish clear expectations and ensure that all employees understand their roles and responsibilities in maintaining compliance with relevant laws, regulations, and internal policies.

The scope of this document includes:

* Overview of the Compliance department's role and responsibilities
* Core policies and standard operating procedures for ensuring compliance
* Guidelines for monitoring and addressing compliance issues
* Penalties and operational governance for non-compliance

# 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures
### Policy 1: Confidentiality and Data Protection

* Purpose: To ensure the confidentiality and protection of sensitive information and data.
* Scope: All employees who handle or have access to confidential or proprietary information.
* Procedure:
	+ Identify and classify sensitive information as confidential or proprietary.
	+ Ensure all electronic and physical storage media containing confidential or proprietary information are securely stored and monitored.
	+ Implement access controls for authorized personnel only.

### Policy 2: Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Know-Your-Customer (KYC)

* Purpose: To prevent money laundering, terrorist financing, and other financial crimes.
* Scope: All employees involved in transactions or customer interactions.
* Procedure:
	+ Verify customer identity and conduct due diligence on new customers.
	+ Monitor and report suspicious transactions to the Compliance department.
	+ Implement controls for cash handling and transactions.

### Policy 3: Record-Keeping and Reporting

* Purpose: To maintain accurate and complete records of business activities.
* Scope: All employees responsible for recording or reporting business activities.
* Procedure:
	+ Maintain accurate and detailed records of all business activities, including financial transactions and customer interactions.
	+ Report any discrepancies or irregularities in records to the Compliance department.

# 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### Compliance Monitoring

* Purpose: To identify and address compliance issues proactively.
* Scope: All employees involved in business activities that may impact compliance.
* Procedure:
	+ Conduct regular audits and reviews of business activities to ensure compliance with policies and procedures.
	+ Identify and report any non-compliance or potential risks to the Compliance department.

### Penalties for Non-Compliance

* Purpose: To deter and address non-compliance in a fair and consistent manner.
* Scope: All employees who violate company policies or procedures.
* Procedure:
	+ Investigate alleged violations of company policies or procedures.
	+ Impose penalties, including disciplinary action, fines, or other corrective measures as necessary.

### Operational Governance

* Purpose: To ensure effective and efficient management of compliance-related activities.
* Scope: Compliance department personnel responsible for managing compliance-related tasks.
* Procedure:
	+ Establish clear roles and responsibilities for compliance-related activities.
	+ Develop and maintain policies, procedures, and training programs to support compliance efforts.
	+ Monitor and report on compliance metrics and key performance indicators.

By following the guidelines and procedures outlined in this document, [Company Name] can ensure continued compliance with relevant laws, regulations, and internal policies, while maintaining a strong commitment to transparency, integrity, and accountability.
