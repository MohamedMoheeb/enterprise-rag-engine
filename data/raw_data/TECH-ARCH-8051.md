### 1. Document Scope and Executive Summary

This Architecture Reference Document (ARD) outlines the core policies, standard operating procedures, and governance framework for the Architecture department at [Company Name]. The purpose of this document is to provide a comprehensive overview of the department's responsibilities, roles, and best practices in supporting the organization's strategic objectives.

The ARD serves as a centralized resource for architects, engineers, and other stakeholders involved in architecture-related activities. It outlines the principles, guidelines, and procedures for designing, developing, and implementing architectures that meet the company's technical, business, and regulatory requirements.

### 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

#### 2.1 Architecture Development Life Cycle

* The architecture development life cycle consists of five phases: planning, design, implementation, testing, and deployment.
* Each phase is governed by specific procedures and guidelines outlined in this document.

#### 2.2 Architecture Review Board (ARB)

* The ARB is responsible for reviewing and approving all architecture-related proposals, designs, and changes.
* The ARB consists of representatives from various departments, including Architecture, Engineering, IT, and Business Operations.

#### 2.3 Change Management

* All changes to existing architectures or the introduction of new architectures must be submitted through the company's change management process.
* Changes are reviewed and approved by the ARB before implementation.

#### 2.4 Documentation and Record-Keeping

* Architects are responsible for maintaining accurate and up-to-date documentation of all architecture-related activities, including design decisions, assumptions, and constraints.
* Documentation is stored in a centralized repository accessible to authorized personnel.

### 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

#### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

* The Architecture department will conduct regular compliance monitoring to ensure adherence to established policies and procedures.
* Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment.

#### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

* Failure to comply with this ARD may result in penalties, including:
	+ Written warnings
	+ Suspension or termination of employment
	+ Mandatory training or education programs

#### 3.3 Operational Governance

* The Architecture department will maintain a governance framework that ensures the effective management and oversight of architecture-related activities.
* This framework includes regular meetings with stakeholders, progress reporting, and issue resolution.
