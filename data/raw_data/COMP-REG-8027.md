## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Data Protection Policy

* Purpose: Ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of sensitive information.
* Scope: All employees, contractors, and third-party vendors who process or have access to sensitive information.
* Procedure:
	+ Handle sensitive information with care and attention.
	+ Use approved encryption methods for transmitting sensitive data.
	+ Regularly back up critical systems and data.

### 2.2 Anti-Corruption Policy

* Purpose: Prevent corrupt practices and ensure fair business dealings.
* Scope: All employees, contractors, and third-party vendors who interact with or process sensitive information on behalf of [Company Name].
* Procedure:
	+ Refrain from engaging in corrupt practices or offering bribes.
	+ Report any suspected corrupt activities to the Compliance department.

### 2.3 Information Security Policy

* Purpose: Protect against unauthorized access, disclosure, disruption, modification, or destruction of sensitive information.
* Scope: All employees, contractors, and third-party vendors who process or have access to sensitive information.
* Procedure:
	+ Use strong passwords and keep them confidential.
	+ Regularly update software and operating systems to ensure security patches are applied.

### 2.4 Record-Keeping Policy

* Purpose: Maintain accurate and transparent records of business transactions and activities.
* Scope: All employees, contractors, and third-party vendors who process or have access to sensitive information.
* Procedure:
	+ Keep accurate and detailed records of all business transactions and activities.
	+ Retain records for a minimum of [X] years.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring:

* Purpose: Monitor compliance with core policies and SOPs.
* Scope: All employees, contractors, and third-party vendors who process or have access to sensitive information.
* Procedure:
	+ Regularly review logs and system activity for suspicious behavior.
	+ Conduct regular audits and risk assessments.

### 3.2 Penalties:

* Purpose: Enforce compliance with core policies and SOPs.
* Scope: All employees, contractors, and third-party vendors who process or have access to sensitive information.
* Procedure:
	+ Imposed penalties will be proportionate to the severity of the breach.
	+ Penalties may include disciplinary action, suspension, or termination.

### 3.3 Operational Governance:

* Purpose: Ensure effective governance and oversight of compliance-related activities.
* Scope: All employees, contractors, and third-party vendors who process or have access to sensitive information.
* Procedure:
	+ Establish a Compliance Committee to oversee compliance efforts.
	+ Provide regular training and awareness programs for employees.
