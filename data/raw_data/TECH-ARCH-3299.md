## 1. Document Scope and Executive Summary

This Architecture Reference Document (ARD) outlines the core policies, standard operating procedures, and compliance monitoring processes for the Architecture department at [Company Name]. The ARD provides a comprehensive guide for architects, engineers, and other stakeholders to ensure consistent application of architectural principles, standards, and best practices across all projects.

The scope of this document includes:

* Defining the architecture decision-making process
* Outlining the roles and responsibilities of architecture team members
* Describing the architecture review and approval process
* Providing guidelines for architecture documentation and artifact management

## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Architecture Decision-Making Process

The architecture decision-making process involves the following steps:

1. **Architecture Request**: Initiators submit requests for new or modified architectures to the Architecture department.
2. **Architecture Review**: The Architecture team reviews the request, assessing feasibility, alignment with company goals, and potential impact on existing systems.
3. **Architecture Design**: The selected architect designs a solution, considering constraints, trade-offs, and stakeholder input.
4. **Architecture Approval**: The designed architecture is reviewed and approved by the Architecture department and relevant stakeholders.
5. **Architecture Implementation**: The approved architecture is implemented, with ongoing monitoring and maintenance.

### 2.2 Roles and Responsibilities

* **Architects**: Responsible for designing, developing, and maintaining architectures; collaborating with stakeholders; and ensuring compliance with company standards.
* **Engineering Teams**: Implement the approved architectures, working closely with architects to ensure successful integration.
* **Stakeholders**: Provide input on architecture requirements, review designs, and approve or reject proposed architectures.

### 2.3 Architecture Review and Approval Process

The architecture review and approval process involves the following steps:

1. **Architecture Submission**: Architects submit their designed architectures for review.
2. **Peer Review**: The submitted architecture is reviewed by a peer architect, ensuring compliance with company standards and best practices.
3. **Stakeholder Input**: Stakeholders provide input on the proposed architecture, addressing concerns and questions.
4. **Final Approval**: The approved architecture is finalized, and implementation begins.

### 2.4 Architecture Documentation and Artifact Management

* **Architecture Artifacts**: Include design documents, diagrams, and other relevant materials that describe the architecture.
* **Artifact Management**: Ensure artifacts are properly version-controlled, backed up, and stored in a centralized repository for future reference and auditing purposes.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

The Architecture department will monitor compliance with company standards and best practices through:

* Regular architecture reviews
* Audits of architecture artifacts
* Stakeholder feedback and input

### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

In cases where non-compliance is detected, the following penalties may apply:

* **Warning**: A written warning to the architect or engineering team.
* **Corrective Action**: The architect or engineering team must implement corrective measures within a specified timeframe.
* **Re-training**: The architect or engineering team must complete re-training on company standards and best practices.

### 3.3 Operational Governance

The Architecture department will:

* Establish a governance framework for architecture decisions
* Define roles and responsibilities for architecture decision-making
* Develop and maintain architecture policies, procedures, and guidelines

By following this Architecture Reference Document, the Architecture department aims to ensure consistent application of architectural principles, standards, and best practices across all projects, thereby maintaining the company's competitive advantage in the market.
