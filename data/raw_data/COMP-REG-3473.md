## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring Program

The Compliance department will conduct regular monitoring and audits to ensure that company policies are being followed and regulatory requirements are met. This includes reviewing financial records, transaction data, and other relevant information.

### 3.2 Penalty Framework

In the event of non-compliance, the following penalty framework will be applied:

* First-time violators: Written warning and mandatory training
* Repeat offenders: Suspension or termination of employment/contract
* Willful violators: Termination of employment/contract and potential legal action

### 3.3 Operational Governance

The Compliance department will work closely with other departments to ensure that compliance policies are integrated into business operations. This includes:

* Providing training and support to employees
* Conducting regular audits and monitoring
* Developing and implementing new policies and procedures as needed
* Maintaining accurate records of compliance-related activities
