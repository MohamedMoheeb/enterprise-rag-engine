## 1. Document Scope and Executive Summary

This document outlines the core policies, standard operating procedures, and compliance monitoring guidelines for the Architecture department. The scope of this document includes all architectural design, development, and maintenance activities within the organization.

The executive summary highlights the importance of adhering to these guidelines in ensuring the effective delivery of high-quality architecture solutions that meet business requirements and regulatory standards.

## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1. Design Principles

* The Architecture department shall adhere to the following design principles:
	+ Reusability: Promote reusable components and services to reduce duplication of effort.
	+ Flexibility: Design architecture that is adaptable to changing business requirements.
	+ Scalability: Ensure architecture can scale to meet growing demands.
	+ Security: Prioritize security in all architectural designs.

### 2.2. Development Process

* The Architecture department shall follow a structured development process, including:
	1. Requirements gathering and analysis
	2. Design creation and review
	3. Implementation and testing
	4. Deployment and maintenance
* All architecture design work shall be documented using standardized templates and tools.

### 2.3. Change Management

* The Architecture department shall have a formal change management process in place, including:
	1. Request submission and evaluation
	2. Impact assessment and risk analysis
	3. Approval and implementation
	4. Post-implementation review and retrospection

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1. Compliance Monitoring

* The Architecture department shall conduct regular compliance monitoring to ensure adherence to established policies and procedures.
* A dedicated compliance officer shall be responsible for ensuring that all architecture-related activities are in compliance with relevant regulations and standards.

### 3.2. Penalties

* Failure to comply with established policies and procedures may result in:
	+ Verbal warnings
	+ Written warnings
	+ Suspension or termination of architecture design work
	+ Revocation of architecture certification

### 3.3. Operational Governance

* The Architecture department shall have a formal governance structure in place, including:
	1. A governing body responsible for setting architecture strategy and direction
	2. A technical review committee responsible for reviewing and approving architecture designs
	3. A quality assurance team responsible for ensuring adherence to established standards and procedures
