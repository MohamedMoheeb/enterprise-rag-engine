## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Employee Onboarding Procedure

The employee onboarding process consists of the following steps:

1. Job offer acceptance: The new hire accepts the job offer via email or phone.
2. Pre-employment screening: The HR department conducts a thorough background check, including verification of education and employment history.
3. New hire paperwork: The new hire completes necessary paperwork, including tax forms and benefits enrollment.
4. Orientation: The new hire attends an orientation session, covering company policies, procedures, and expectations.

### 2.2 Performance Management Procedure

The performance management process consists of the following steps:

1. Goal setting: Employees set performance goals with their supervisors during quarterly or annual reviews.
2. Regular check-ins: Supervisors conduct regular check-ins to monitor progress and provide feedback.
3. Performance evaluation: Employees receive a formal performance evaluation, highlighting strengths and areas for improvement.

### 2.3 Termination Procedure

The termination procedure consists of the following steps:

1. Verbal warning: The employee receives a verbal warning for performance or disciplinary issues.
2. Written warning: The employee receives a written warning outlining specific expectations for improvement.
3. Termination notice: The employee is terminated, with a minimum 30-day notice period.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Labor Laws and Regulations

The HR department ensures compliance with all applicable labor laws and regulations, including:

* Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA)
* Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA)
* Age Discrimination in Employment Act (ADEA)

### 3.2 Industry Standards and Best Practices

The HR department adheres to industry standards and best practices for human resources management, including:

* Society for Human Resource Management (SHRM) guidelines
* Equal Employment Opportunity Commission (EEOC) regulations

### 3.3 Penalties for Non-Compliance

 Failure to comply with labor laws, regulations, or industry standards may result in penalties, including:

* Fines and fees
* Legal action
* Reputational damage

### 3.4 Operational Governance

The HR department is responsible for ensuring the effective governance of all HR-related activities, including:

* Policy development and implementation
* Procedure establishment and enforcement
* Compliance monitoring and reporting
