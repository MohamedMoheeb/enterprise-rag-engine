# ARCHITECTURE REFERENCE DOCUMENT: TECH-ARCH-3427
## Department: Architecture
## Author: Julie Clark
## Effective Date: 2022-02-24

### Document Scope and Executive Summary
This document outlines the core policies, standard operating procedures, and compliance guidelines for the Architecture department. The purpose of this reference document is to provide a comprehensive framework for architects, designers, and stakeholders to ensure consistency, quality, and adherence to organizational standards.

The Architecture department plays a critical role in shaping our company's digital landscape by designing and developing innovative solutions that meet business needs and customer expectations. This document aims to establish clear guidelines for architectural decision-making, collaboration, and communication within the organization.

### Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures
#### Policy 1: Architectural Decision-Making

* Architects shall follow a structured approach to make informed design decisions, considering factors such as scalability, security, performance, and maintainability.
* All architectural designs must be reviewed and approved by senior architects or architecture leads before implementation.

#### Policy 2: Collaboration and Communication

* Architects are expected to collaborate with cross-functional teams, including developers, product owners, and stakeholders, to ensure seamless integration of their designs into the overall system.
* Regular meetings and updates shall be maintained to keep all stakeholders informed about design progress and changes.

#### Policy 3: Code Quality and Best Practices

* Architects must adhere to established coding standards, following best practices for code organization, naming conventions, and commenting.
* All code written by architects shall be reviewed and approved by peers or senior architects before deployment.

### Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance
#### Compliance Monitoring

* The Architecture department will conduct regular audits to ensure adherence to the policies outlined in this document.
* Non-compliance may result in rework, additional training, or disciplinary action as deemed necessary.

#### Penalties for Non-Compliance

* First-time offenders: written warning and additional training
* Second-time offenders: one-day suspension without pay
* Repeated offenders: termination of employment

#### Operational Governance

* The Architecture department will maintain a centralized repository for architectural designs, standards, and guidelines.
* All architects shall participate in regular peer reviews and knowledge-sharing sessions to ensure knowledge transfer and skill development.

This reference document serves as a living document, subject to change as the organization evolves. Any updates or changes will be communicated through the standard change management process.
