* Branding and visual identity guidelines

# 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

The Architecture department will conduct regular compliance monitoring to ensure adherence to organizational policies, standards, and regulatory requirements.

* Quarterly reviews of architectural designs and deliverables
* Annual audits of architectural processes and procedures
* Regular training and awareness programs for architects and stakeholders

### 3.2 Penalties and Consequences

Non-compliance with organizational policies, standards, or regulatory requirements may result in penalties or consequences, including:

* Verbal warnings or written reprimands
* Mandatory training or education programs
* Suspension or termination of architectural services
* Financial penalties or fines

The Architecture department will maintain a governance structure to ensure operational efficiency and effectiveness.

* A designated architecture lead will oversee the department's operations and strategy
* Regular meetings with stakeholders and team members will be conducted
* Clear decision-making frameworks and escalation procedures will be established
