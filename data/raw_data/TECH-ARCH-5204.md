## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Architectural Principles

The Architecture department follows a set of core principles that guide the design and development of architecture solutions:

* Scalability: Architecture solutions must be designed to scale efficiently and effectively.
* Flexibility: Solutions should be flexible enough to accommodate changing business needs and technological advancements.
* Security: Architecture solutions must prioritize security and data protection.
* Interoperability: Solutions should be designed to integrate seamlessly with existing systems and technologies.

### 2.2 Design Standards

The Architecture department adheres to the following design standards:

* [Company Name]'s Brand Guidelines
* Industry-standard design patterns and best practices
* Compliance with relevant regulations and standards (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA)

### 2.3 Development Processes

The Architecture department follows a structured development process that includes:

1. Requirements gathering and analysis
2. Design and prototyping
3. Development and testing
4. Quality assurance and validation
5. Deployment and maintenance

### 2.4 Change Management

The Architecture department implements change management processes to ensure that changes are properly assessed, approved, and implemented. This includes:

* Request for Change (RFC) process
* Impact analysis and risk assessment
* Approval and implementation planning

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

The Architecture department is responsible for monitoring compliance with company policies, industry regulations, and standards.

### 3.2 Penalties and Corrective Actions

In the event of non-compliance or deviation from established procedures, the following penalties and corrective actions will be implemented:

* Verbal warning
* Written warning
* Suspension of access to architecture tools and systems
* Termination of employment (in extreme cases)

### 3.3 Operational Governance

The Architecture department operates under a governance framework that includes:

* Clear roles and responsibilities for architects, designers, and stakeholders
* Regular meetings and reporting to ensure transparency and accountability
* Continuous training and professional development to stay up-to-date with industry trends and best practices
