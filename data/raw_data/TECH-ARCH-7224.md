3. **Change Approval**: Approved changes will be documented and tracked.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

The Architecture department will monitor compliance with this document using the following procedures:

1. **Regular Audits**: The Architecture team will conduct regular audits to ensure adherence to standards and guidelines.
2. **Compliance Reports**: The Architecture team will provide quarterly compliance reports to senior management.

### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

The Architecture department reserves the right to impose penalties for non-compliance with this document, including:

1. **Verbal Warning**: A verbal warning will be issued for minor infractions.
2. **Written Warning**: A written warning will be issued for moderate infractions.
3. **Project Suspension**: Project suspension will be imposed for severe infractions.

### 3.3 Operational Governance

The Architecture department will operate according to the following governance structure:

1. **Architecture Steering Committee**: The committee will oversee architecture strategy, standards, and best practices.
2. **Architecture Review Board**: The board will review and approve all architecture designs.

This document serves as a living reference for the Architecture department. It is essential that all team members understand their roles and responsibilities in adhering to these policies and procedures.
