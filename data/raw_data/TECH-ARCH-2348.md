# TECH-ARCH-2348: Architecture Reference Document
## Department: Architecture
## Author: Joseph Parker
## Effective Date: 2020-07-07

# 1. Document Scope and Executive Summary
This reference document outlines the core policies, standard operating procedures, and governance framework for the Architecture department within our organization. The purpose of this document is to provide a comprehensive guide for architects, project managers, and stakeholders on the principles, guidelines, and best practices for designing, developing, and delivering high-quality architectural solutions.

The scope of this document includes:

* Defining the architecture governance framework
* Establishing standard operating procedures for design, development, and testing
* Outlining compliance monitoring and penalty structures
* Describing operational governance and communication protocols

# 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Design Principles

Our architecture team shall adhere to the following design principles:

* Scalability: Designs should be scalable to accommodate growth and changing business needs.
* Flexibility: Architectures should be flexible to adapt to new technologies, tools, and processes.
* Security: Designs should prioritize security and data protection.
* Usability: Architectures should ensure ease of use for end-users.

### 2.2 Development Processes

1. **Project Initiation**: Projects shall start with a clear understanding of project goals, objectives, and scope.
2. **Requirements Gathering**: Architects shall gather and document requirements through stakeholder interviews, workshops, and surveys.
3. **Design Phase**: Architects shall create detailed designs, including technical specifications, wireframes, and prototypes.
4. **Development Phase**: Developers shall implement the design, adhering to coding standards and best practices.
5. **Testing Phase**: Testing shall be conducted to ensure the solution meets requirements and is free from defects.
6. **Deployment Phase**: The solution shall be deployed to production, with monitoring and maintenance procedures established.

### 2.3 Change Management

1. **Request for Change (RFC)**: Changes shall be requested through an RFC form, outlining the reason, impact, and proposed solution.
2. **Change Evaluation**: The architecture team shall evaluate the RFC, considering factors such as feasibility, risk, and business value.
3. **Change Implementation**: Approved changes shall be implemented, with documentation of the change and its rationale.

# 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

The architecture department shall monitor compliance with this reference document through:

* Regular audits and reviews
* Incident reporting and tracking
* Quarterly reports to management and stakeholders

### 3.2 Penalties

In the event of non-compliance, the following penalties may apply:

* Warning: A written warning shall be issued, outlining the non-compliance and corrective actions.
* Suspension: The architect's access to sensitive systems or data shall be suspended for a specified period.
* Termination: In severe cases, termination of employment or contract may occur.

### 3.3 Operational Governance

The architecture department shall operate under the following governance structure:

1. **Architecture Committee**: A committee comprising senior architects and stakeholders shall oversee architecture decisions and ensure alignment with organizational goals.
2. **Design Authority**: The design authority shall be responsible for reviewing and approving architectural designs, ensuring compliance with this reference document.
3. **Communication Protocols**: Regular communication protocols shall be established to keep stakeholders informed of project progress, changes, and issues.

By adhering to the principles, policies, and procedures outlined in this reference document, the Architecture department aims to deliver high-quality solutions that meet business needs while ensuring compliance with organizational standards and regulatory requirements.
