# TECH-ARCH-2197: Architecture Reference Document
## Department: Architecture
## Author: Melissa Porter
## Effective Date: 2021-05-17

### Document Scope and Executive Summary

This reference document outlines the core policies, standard operating procedures, and governance framework for the Architecture department. The purpose of this document is to provide a comprehensive guide for architects, engineers, and other stakeholders involved in the design, development, and maintenance of our company's technical infrastructure.

The scope of this document includes:

* Design principles and guidelines
* Software architecture standards
* Infrastructure management procedures
* Collaboration and communication protocols

### 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

#### 2.1 Architecture Governance

The Architecture department is responsible for ensuring that all technical systems, solutions, and infrastructure align with the company's overall strategy and goals. To achieve this, we will:

* Develop and maintain a comprehensive architecture roadmap
* Conduct regular technology assessments and gap analyses
* Provide architectural guidance and oversight to project teams

#### 2.2 Design Principles

Our design principles are based on the following core values:

* Scalability: Designs should be able to scale horizontally or vertically as needed.
* Security: Designs should prioritize security and confidentiality of data.
* Flexibility: Designs should allow for flexibility in terms of technology stacks, integrations, and future changes.

#### 2.3 Software Architecture Standards

We will adhere to the following software architecture standards:

* Use of microservices-based architectures
* Adherence to RESTful API design principles
* Utilization of containerization (e.g., Docker) and orchestration (e.g., Kubernetes)

### 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

#### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

The Architecture department will conduct regular compliance monitoring to ensure that all technical systems, solutions, and infrastructure meet the company's security and regulatory requirements.

#### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

 Failure to comply with architecture standards and policies may result in:

* Delayed project approvals
* Additional training or certification requirements
* Suspension or revocation of access to sensitive information or technical systems

#### 3.3 Operational Governance

The Architecture department will maintain a centralized governance framework, including:

* A comprehensive architecture repository (e.g., Confluence)
* Regular architecture review meetings with stakeholders
* Continuous integration and continuous deployment (CI/CD) pipelines for automated testing and deployment
