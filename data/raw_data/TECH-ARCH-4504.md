# TECH-ARCH-4504: Architecture Reference Document
## Department: Architecture
## Author: Isaiah Li
## Effective Date: 2021-06-26

### Executive Summary

This document outlines the scope, policies, and standard operating procedures for the Architecture department. The purpose of this reference is to provide a comprehensive guide for architects, engineers, and other stakeholders involved in designing and implementing technology solutions within the organization.

### Document Scope

This document applies to all architecture team members, including architects, senior architects, and architecture leads. It covers the following key areas:

* Design principles and standards
* Technology stack guidelines
* Collaboration and communication protocols
* Change management procedures
* Compliance monitoring and reporting

### 1. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

#### 1.1 Architecture Principles

The organization's architecture principles serve as a foundation for all technology design decisions. These principles include:

* Scalability: Designs should be scalable to meet future business needs.
* Flexibility: Solutions should be flexible enough to adapt to changing requirements.
* Security: Confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data must be ensured at all times.
* Interoperability: Systems should be designed to work seamlessly with other internal and external systems.

#### 1.2 Technology Stack Guidelines

The organization has adopted a standardized technology stack for all projects. The current stack includes:

* Front-end: React, Angular, or Vue.js
* Back-end: Node.js, .NET Core, or Python
* Database: Relational databases (e.g., MySQL) or NoSQL databases (e.g., MongoDB)
* Infrastructure: Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, or Google Cloud Platform (GCP)

#### 1.3 Collaboration and Communication Protocols

The architecture team will utilize the following collaboration tools and protocols:

* Version control system: Git
* Issue tracking system: Jira
* Project management tool: Asana or Trello
* Regular meetings: Bi-weekly architecture reviews, monthly project status updates

### 2. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

#### 2.1 Compliance Monitoring

The architecture team will conduct regular compliance monitoring to ensure adherence to organizational policies, industry standards, and regulatory requirements.

* Quarterly security assessments
* Annual audits by internal or external auditors
* Continuous monitoring of technology infrastructure and systems

#### 2.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

Failure to comply with architectural guidelines, policies, and procedures may result in the following penalties:

* Verbal warnings
* Written warnings
* Suspension or termination of architecture privileges
* Mandatory training or education programs

#### 2.3 Operational Governance

The architecture team will adhere to the following operational governance principles:

* Clear roles and responsibilities
* Defined decision-making processes
* Regular status updates and progress reports
* Continuous improvement and knowledge sharing within the team

### Conclusion

This document outlines the core policies, standard operating procedures, and compliance monitoring framework for the Architecture department. By adhering to these guidelines, architecture team members can ensure that technology solutions are designed and implemented in a way that aligns with organizational goals and values.
