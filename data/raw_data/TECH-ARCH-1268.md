# TECH-ARCH-1268: Architecture Reference Document
## Department: Architecture
## Author: Rebecca Mullen
## Effective Date: 2020-03-27

# 1. Document Scope and Executive Summary
This reference document outlines the Architecture department's policies, procedures, and guidelines for designing, developing, and maintaining software architectures that align with the company's overall strategy and vision. The purpose of this document is to provide a comprehensive guide for architects, engineers, and other stakeholders involved in architecture development.

The scope of this document includes:

* Defining the role and responsibilities of the Architecture department
* Outlining the core policies and standard operating procedures for architecture development
* Establishing compliance monitoring, penalties, and operational governance guidelines

# 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Design Principles

The Architecture department will adhere to the following design principles:

* **Simplicity**: Designs should be simple, easy to understand, and maintainable.
* **Flexibility**: Designs should be flexible enough to accommodate changing business requirements.
* **Scalability**: Designs should scale horizontally or vertically as needed.
* **Security**: Designs should prioritize security and data protection.

### 2.2 Architecture Development Process

The architecture development process consists of the following stages:

1. **Requirements Gathering**: Gather and document business requirements from stakeholders.
2. **Architecture Definition**: Define the software architecture, including technology selection, component interactions, and scalability considerations.
3. **Design Review**: Conduct a design review to ensure compliance with company standards and best practices.
4. **Implementation**: Implement the architecture using approved technologies and tools.
5. **Testing and Validation**: Test and validate the architecture against defined requirements and acceptance criteria.

### 2.3 Architecture Governance

The Architecture department will govern architecture development through the following mechanisms:

* **Architecture Committee**: A committee composed of architects, engineers, and stakeholders to review and approve architecture designs.
* **Change Management Process**: A process for managing changes to existing architectures or introducing new architectures.

# 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

The Architecture department will monitor compliance with this reference document through:

* **Regular Audits**: Conduct regular audits of architecture development processes and designs.
* **Architecture Committee Reviews**: Review architecture designs and changes to ensure compliance.

### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

In the event of non-compliance, penalties may include:

* **Warning**: A written warning to the architect or team responsible.
* **Corrective Action**: Require corrective action to address the non-compliance issue.
* **Reprimand**: A formal reprimand to the architect or team responsible.

### 3.3 Operational Governance

The Architecture department will govern its operations through:

* **Architecture Department Meetings**: Regular meetings to discuss architecture-related topics and share knowledge.
* **Document Control**: Maintain a controlled repository of architecture documents, including design principles, architecture designs, and technical standards.

By following this reference document, the Architecture department aims to ensure that software architectures are designed and developed in accordance with company standards and best practices, ultimately driving business success.
