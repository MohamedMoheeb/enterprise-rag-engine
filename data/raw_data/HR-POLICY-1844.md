## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Employee Relations Policy

* Purpose: To promote a positive work environment and resolve conflicts in a fair and timely manner.
* Scope: All employees are covered by this policy.
* Procedure:
	+ Identify the issue and gather relevant facts.
	+ Conduct an investigation, if necessary.
	+ Document all findings and discussions.
	+ Provide a resolution or recommendation to the involved parties.

### 2.2 Recruitment and Hiring Policy

* Purpose: To ensure fair and unbiased hiring practices that attract top talent.
* Scope: All job openings are covered by this policy.
* Procedure:
	+ Define job requirements and qualifications.
	+ Advertise job openings internally and externally.
	+ Review applications and conduct interviews.
	+ Verify candidate credentials and references.

### 2.3 Employee Training and Development Policy

* Purpose: To enhance employee skills, knowledge, and performance.
* Scope: All employees are eligible for training and development opportunities.
* Procedure:
	+ Identify training needs and develop a plan.
	+ Offer various training formats (e.g., online courses, workshops).
	+ Track participant progress and evaluate program effectiveness.

### 2.4 Compensation and Benefits Policy

* Purpose: To provide fair compensation and benefits that attract and retain top talent.
* Scope: All employees are covered by this policy.
* Procedure:
	+ Conduct regular salary surveys and market research.
	+ Develop a compensation plan based on performance, experience, and job requirements.
	+ Offer a range of benefits (e.g., health insurance, retirement plans).

### 2.5 Termination Policy

* Purpose: To ensure fair and timely termination procedures that minimize disruption to the business.
* Scope: All employees are covered by this policy.
* Procedure:
	+ Document performance issues and conduct regular reviews.
	+ Provide progressive discipline (e.g., verbal warnings, written warnings).
	+ Offer severance packages or outplacement assistance.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

* Purpose: To ensure ongoing compliance with relevant laws, regulations, and company policies.
* Scope: All HR-related activities are covered by this process.
* Procedure:
	+ Conduct regular audits and reviews.
	+ Identify and address non-compliance issues promptly.
	+ Document all findings and corrective actions.

### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

* Purpose: To deter non-compliance and ensure accountability.
* Scope: All employees are covered by this policy.
* Procedure:
	+ Implement progressive penalties (e.g., verbal warnings, written warnings).
	+ Document all incidents of non-compliance.

### 3.3 Operational Governance

* Purpose: To ensure effective HR operations that support business objectives.
* Scope: All HR-related activities are covered by this process.
* Procedure:
	+ Establish clear roles and responsibilities.
	+ Develop and maintain relevant policies and procedures.
	+ Monitor and evaluate HR performance regularly.
