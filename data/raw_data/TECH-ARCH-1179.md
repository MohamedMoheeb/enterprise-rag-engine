## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Design Standards and Principles

The Architecture department follows a set of design standards and principles to ensure consistency across all projects:

* Use of [Brand/Style Guide] for visual identity
* Adherence to [Industry/Company-specific] guidelines and regulations

### 2.2 Workflow and Collaboration

The Architecture department operates within the following workflow and collaboration framework:

* Project initiation: Request for proposal (RFP) or project charter submission
* Design development: Conceptualization, prototyping, and refinement
* Review and approval: Stakeholder feedback, design iteration, and final sign-off
* Implementation and deployment: Collaboration with development teams and IT operations

### 2.3 Communication Protocols

The Architecture department uses the following communication protocols:

* Regular project meetings: Bi-weekly or weekly depending on project scope and complexity
* Design reviews: Scheduled reviews for feedback, design iteration, and quality control
* Ad-hoc communication: Email, instant messaging, or video conferencing as needed

### 2.4 Documentation and Version Control

The Architecture department maintains accurate documentation and version control through:

* Version-controlled design files (e.g., Sketch, Figma, or Adobe Creative Cloud)
* Documentation templates for project proposals, designs, and reports
* Regular backups and archiving of design files and documentation

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

The Architecture department ensures compliance with:

* Verbal warnings or written reprimands for minor infractions

The Architecture department operates under the following governance structure:

* Department head: Responsible for overall strategy, direction, and resource allocation
* Team leads: Oversees specific projects, teams, or areas of expertise
* Architects and designers: Focus on design development, implementation, and maintenance
