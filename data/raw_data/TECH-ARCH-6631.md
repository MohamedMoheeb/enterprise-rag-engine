# Architecture Reference Document: TECH-ARCH-6631
## Department: Architecture
## Author: Mark Cannon
## Effective Date: 2020-09-22


### 1. Document Scope and Executive Summary

The purpose of this document is to establish a comprehensive reference guide for the Architecture department, outlining core policies, standard operating procedures, and guidelines for ensuring effective design, development, and deployment of enterprise architecture solutions.

This document serves as a foundation for architects, engineers, and other stakeholders within the organization, providing a shared understanding of architectural principles, standards, and best practices. By adhering to these guidelines, we aim to ensure the delivery of high-quality, scalable, and maintainable architectures that support business objectives.

### 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

#### 2.1. Architecture Principles

The following core principles guide our architecture decisions:

* **Separation of Concerns**: Logical separation of concerns into layers or tiers
* **Modularity**: Design for modularity, reusability, and maintainability
* **Scalability**: Ensure architectures are scalable to meet evolving business needs
* **Security**: Prioritize security throughout the design process

#### 2.2. Standardized Architecture Framework

We adhere to a standardized architecture framework, comprising the following components:

* **Business Layer**: Defines business logic and rules
* **Integration Layer**: Handles data integration and API management
* **Presentation Layer**: Delivers user interfaces and experiences
* **Data Storage**: Manages data storage and retrieval

#### 2.3. Change Management Process

The Architecture department follows a structured change management process, involving:

1. **Change Request**: Submit a formal request for architectural changes or enhancements
2. **Assessment**: Evaluate the impact of proposed changes on existing architectures
3. **Design**: Develop a detailed design and implementation plan
4. **Implementation**: Execute the planned changes and monitor results
5. **Review**: Conduct post-implementation reviews to assess effectiveness and identify opportunities for improvement

### 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

#### 3.1. Compliance Monitoring

The Architecture department conducts regular compliance monitoring to ensure adherence to established policies and procedures.

* **Audits**: Perform periodic audits to verify conformance with architecture standards
* **Risk Assessments**: Identify potential risks and develop mitigation strategies

#### 3.2. Penalties for Non-Compliance

In the event of non-compliance, the following penalties may apply:

* **Warning**: Issued for minor infractions or first-time offenses
* **Corrective Action**: Requires implementation of corrective measures to address specific issues
* **Penalty**: Imposes financial or other consequences for repeated or egregious violations

#### 3.3. Operational Governance

The Architecture department operates under the following governance structure:

* **Architecture Committee**: Oversees architecture decisions, provides strategic guidance, and ensures alignment with business objectives
* **Project Management Office (PMO)**: Manages project execution, tracks progress, and ensures compliance with architectural standards

By adhering to this reference document, we ensure a consistent and effective approach to enterprise architecture, enabling the organization to achieve its goals and maintain a competitive edge.
