# TECH-ARCH-3828
# Architecture Reference Document

## Department: Architecture
## Author: Teresa Baker
## Effective Date: 2021-04-08

### Document Scope and Executive Summary
This document outlines the core policies, standard operating procedures (SOPs), and guidelines for the Architecture department. The purpose of this reference guide is to provide a comprehensive framework for architects, designers, and stakeholders to ensure consistency, quality, and compliance in all architectural-related activities.

### Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

#### 1. Design Standards
The Architecture department will adhere to the following design standards:

* All designs must be created using approved software tools (e.g., Autodesk Revit, SketchUp).
* Designs must comply with company branding guidelines (logos, color schemes, typography).
* All designs should be visually appealing and easy to navigate.

#### 2. Project Management
Project management best practices will be followed for all architectural projects:

* Projects will be assigned a unique identifier and tracked using project management software.
* Timelines, milestones, and deliverables will be clearly defined and communicated to stakeholders.
* Regular progress reports and updates will be provided to ensure transparency.

#### 3. Collaboration and Communication
Effective communication is crucial for successful collaboration:

* Architects will maintain open lines of communication with internal stakeholders (e.g., project managers, engineers).
* Design reviews and feedback sessions will be conducted regularly.
* Documentation of design decisions, assumptions, and constraints will be maintained.

### Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

#### 1. Compliance Monitoring
The Architecture department will conduct regular compliance checks to ensure adherence to:

* Company policies and procedures.
* Industry standards (e.g., ASHRAE, IBC).
* Local building codes and regulations.

#### 2. Penalties for Non-Compliance
Failure to comply with these guidelines may result in:

* Verbal warnings or written reprimands.
* Suspension of design privileges.
* Termination of employment in extreme cases.

#### 3. Operational Governance
The Architecture department will operate under the following governance structure:

* A dedicated Architecture Steering Committee will oversee department operations and ensure compliance with this reference document.
* The committee will consist of representatives from various departments (e.g., engineering, facilities management).
* Regular meetings and reporting will be conducted to ensure transparency and accountability.

By adhering to these guidelines, the Architecture department aims to maintain its commitment to excellence in design, collaboration, and compliance.
