## 1. Document Scope and Executive Summary

This reference document outlines the policies, procedures, and guidelines for the Architecture department at [Company Name]. The purpose of this document is to provide a comprehensive framework for architects to follow when designing, developing, and maintaining software systems, architecture models, and technical solutions.

### Scope

This document applies to all employees working in the Architecture department, including architects, senior architects, team leads, and other supporting staff. It covers the following areas:

* Software design and development
* Architecture modeling and documentation
* Technical solutioning and implementation
* Collaboration with cross-functional teams

### Executive Summary

The Architecture department is responsible for creating high-quality software systems that meet business requirements and align with company goals. This document outlines the procedures, guidelines, and best practices for architects to follow when working on projects, ensuring consistency, quality, and efficiency in their work.

## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### Software Design and Development

* All software designs and developments must adhere to the [Company Name] Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) framework.
* Architects must use approved design tools and templates for creating software architecture models and documentation.
* Code reviews and peer-to-peer feedback are mandatory for all code changes.

### Architecture Modeling and Documentation

* Architects must use standardized architecture modeling notation (e.g., SysML, BPMN).
* All architecture models and documentation must be reviewed and approved by the Architecture department before being shared with stakeholders or released to production.
* Document templates and formats must be used consistently throughout the organization.

### Technical Solutioning and Implementation

* Architects must participate in project planning and scoping activities to ensure that technical solutions align with business requirements.
* All technical solutions must be thoroughly tested, validated, and verified before being deployed to production.
* Change management procedures must be followed for any changes to existing systems or infrastructure.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### Compliance Monitoring

* The Architecture department will conduct regular audits and reviews to ensure compliance with this document and other relevant policies.
* Architects are responsible for maintaining accurate records of their work, including design decisions, code changes, and testing results.
* The department will provide training and support as needed to help architects comply with these policies.

### Penalties

* Failure to adhere to the procedures outlined in this document may result in:
	+ Verbal warnings
	+ Written warnings
	+ Suspension of architect privileges
	+ Termination of employment (in extreme cases)

### Operational Governance

* The Architecture department will maintain a centralized repository for architecture models, documentation, and technical solutions.
* Architects are expected to participate in regular meetings with the department to discuss project progress, share knowledge, and resolve issues.
* The department will provide regular reporting on architecture-related metrics, such as design quality, code quality, and testing effectiveness.
