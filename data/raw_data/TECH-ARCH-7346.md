## Department: Architecture
## Author: Thomas Browning

### Document Scope and Executive Summary

This Architecture Reference Document (ARD) outlines the guiding principles, standard operating procedures, and compliance framework for the Architecture department. The ARD aims to ensure consistency, efficiency, and high-quality architecture solutions across the organization.

The scope of this document includes:

* Defining architectural standards and best practices
* Establishing guidelines for software development and infrastructure design
* Outlining processes for architecture review, approval, and maintenance
* Providing a framework for compliance with organizational policies and regulatory requirements

### 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

#### 2.1 Architecture Principles

The Architecture department adheres to the following core principles:

* Align with business objectives and strategic initiatives
* Ensure scalability, security, and maintainability of systems
* Prioritize simplicity, modularity, and reusability in design
* Foster collaboration and knowledge sharing across teams

#### 2.2 Architecture Review and Approval Process

1. **Initial Assessment**: Architects submit architecture proposals to the Architecture Review Committee (ARC) for an initial assessment.
2. **Peer Review**: The ARC conducts a peer review of the proposal, providing feedback and suggestions for improvement.
3. **Approval**: The ARC approves or rejects the proposal based on its alignment with organizational goals and adherence to architectural standards.
4. **Documentation**: The approved architecture is documented and maintained in the organization's knowledge management system.

#### 2.3 Software Development Life Cycle

The Architecture department follows a standardized software development life cycle, including:

1. **Requirements Gathering**: Define project requirements and scope
2. **Design**: Develop detailed design specifications
3. **Implementation**: Implement the solution according to design specifications
4. **Testing**: Conduct thorough testing and quality assurance
5. **Deployment**: Deploy the solution in a production environment

### 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

#### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

The Architecture department is responsible for monitoring compliance with organizational policies, regulatory requirements, and industry standards.

* Regularly review and update architecture proposals to ensure compliance
* Conduct audits and assessments to identify potential non-compliance issues

#### 3.2 Penalties and Remediation

In cases of non-compliance, the following penalties and remediation steps will be taken:

* **Warning**: First-time offenders receive a written warning and guidance on corrective actions.
* **Corrective Action Plan**: Repeat offenders are required to develop and implement a Corrective Action Plan (CAP) to address the issue.
* **Penalty**: Failure to comply with CAP may result in disciplinary action, including termination of employment.

#### 3.3 Operational Governance

The Architecture department will:

* Establish clear roles and responsibilities for architecture team members
* Define key performance indicators (KPIs) and metrics for architecture success
* Foster a culture of continuous learning, innovation, and improvement
