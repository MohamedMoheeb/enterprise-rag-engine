## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1. Architecture Standards

The Architecture department adheres to a set of standards that govern the design and implementation of software systems. These standards include:

* Programming languages: Java, Python, and C++
* Development frameworks: Spring, Django, and React
* Database management systems: MySQL, PostgreSQL, and MongoDB
* Cloud platforms: AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud

### 2.2. Development Process

The development process involves the following stages:

1. Requirements gathering and analysis
2. Design and prototyping
3. Implementation and testing
4. Deployment and maintenance

Each stage is governed by a set of procedures and guidelines that ensure the quality and consistency of the software systems.

### 2.3. Collaboration and Communication

The Architecture department promotes collaboration and communication among team members, stakeholders, and other departments through:

* Regular meetings and stand-ups
* Shared project management tools (e.g., Jira, Trello)
* Clear documentation and reporting
* Open-door policy for feedback and suggestions

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1. Compliance Monitoring

The Architecture department is responsible for monitoring compliance with the standards, procedures, and guidelines outlined in this document. This includes:

* Audits and reviews of software systems
* Regular inspections of development processes
* Feedback and suggestions from team members and stakeholders

### 3.2. Penalties

Failure to comply with the standards, procedures, and guidelines may result in penalties, including but not limited to:

* Warning letters
* Performance improvement plans
* Suspension or termination of employment

### 3.3. Operational Governance

The Architecture department is responsible for governing its operations through a set of policies and procedures that ensure the efficiency, effectiveness, and transparency of its activities. This includes:

* Budgeting and financial management
* Human resources and personnel management
* Risk management and mitigation
