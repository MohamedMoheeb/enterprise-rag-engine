# Architecture Reference Document: TECH-ARCH-6250

## 1. Document Scope and Executive Summary

This Architecture Reference Document (ARD) outlines the core policies, standard operating procedures, and governance framework for the Architecture department at [Company Name]. The document aims to provide a comprehensive understanding of the department's objectives, roles, and responsibilities, as well as its relationships with other departments and stakeholders.

The ARD is intended for use by all employees within the Architecture department, as well as other departments and stakeholders who interact with or rely on architecture-related services. This document will serve as a reference guide for architects, engineers, project managers, and other professionals working in the Architecture department.

## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1. Architecture Process Management

* The Architecture department is responsible for developing, maintaining, and improving the company's architectural frameworks, standards, and guidelines.
* Architects will adhere to established architecture development processes, including requirements gathering, design, implementation, testing, and deployment.
* All changes to existing architectures or introduction of new ones must be approved by the Architecture Steering Committee.

### 2.2. Design Principles and Patterns

* The Architecture department will maintain a catalog of approved design principles, patterns, and anti-patterns.
* Architects will adhere to these guidelines when designing and implementing solutions.
* Exceptions to established design principles and patterns must be justified and approved by the Architecture Steering Committee.

### 2.3. Technology Stewardship

* The Architecture department is responsible for monitoring and maintaining the company's technology landscape, including infrastructure, platforms, and tools.
* Architects will identify and prioritize areas for improvement, propose solutions, and collaborate with other departments to implement changes.
* The Architecture Steering Committee will review and approve all major technology decisions.

### 2.4. Collaboration and Communication

* Architects will work closely with cross-functional teams, including development, project management, and product ownership.
* Regular meetings and status updates will be maintained to ensure effective communication and collaboration.
* Architects will participate in architectural reviews and provide input on design decisions affecting the company's technology landscape.

### 2.5. Knowledge Management

* The Architecture department will maintain a knowledge repository containing architecture-related documentation, including design documents, diagrams, and specifications.
* Architects will contribute to and leverage this repository to share knowledge, best practices, and lessons learned.
* The Architecture Steering Committee will review and approve all changes to the knowledge repository.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1. Compliance Monitoring

* The Architecture department will maintain a compliance tracking system to monitor adherence to established policies, procedures, and governance frameworks.
* Architects will be responsible for ensuring their work complies with company standards and guidelines.
* The Architecture Steering Committee will review and address any non-compliance issues.

### 3.2. Penalties and Corrective Actions

* Failure to comply with established policies, procedures, or governance frameworks may result in disciplinary action, including verbal warnings, written warnings, or suspension of architecture-related activities.
* Architects must correct any identified compliance issues within a reasonable timeframe, as determined by the Architecture Steering Committee.

### 3.3. Operational Governance

* The Architecture department will maintain an incident management process to handle architecture-related issues and outages.
* Architects will participate in regular meetings with other departments to discuss architecture-related topics, share knowledge, and address concerns.
* The Architecture Steering Committee will review and approve all major architectural decisions affecting the company's technology landscape.
