Department: Architecture
Author: Gregory Stewart
Effective Date: 2021-02-28

## 1. Document Scope and Executive Summary

This document outlines the core policies, standard operating procedures (SOPs), and governance framework for the Architecture department at [Company Name]. The scope of this document is to provide a comprehensive reference guide for architects, engineers, and other stakeholders involved in the design and development of company projects.

The executive summary provides an overview of the architecture process, highlighting key principles, best practices, and standards. This document aims to ensure consistency, efficiency, and quality in the delivery of architectural services across the organization.

## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1. Design Principles

* The company adopts a holistic approach to design, considering technical, financial, and environmental factors.
* All designs shall conform to industry-recognized standards and best practices.
* Designs shall be reviewed and approved by relevant stakeholders before implementation.

### 2.2. Architecture Review Process

* A peer review process is conducted for all architectural deliverables (e.g., designs, plans, specifications).
* Reviews are performed by designated architects or engineers with expertise in the specific area of focus.
* All reviews will be documented, and feedback provided to the architect/designer.

### 2.3. Change Management

* Changes to existing architectures shall be assessed for potential impact on overall system functionality and performance.
* A change request process is established for evaluating and approving changes.
* The architecture team is responsible for maintaining a comprehensive record of all changes.

### 2.4. Documentation and Record-Keeping

* All architectural deliverables, including designs, plans, specifications, and documentation, shall be maintained in a centralized repository (e.g., project management software, document management system).
* Records will be updated regularly to reflect changes and updates.
* The architecture team is responsible for ensuring the integrity and accessibility of all records.

### 2.5. Collaboration and Communication

* Architects and engineers are expected to collaborate with cross-functional teams (e.g., engineering, operations, project management) throughout the design and development process.
* Regular communication and status updates will be maintained among stakeholders.
* The architecture team is responsible for facilitating collaboration and ensuring effective communication.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1. Compliance Monitoring

* The architecture team shall conduct regular compliance checks to ensure adherence to established policies, SOPs, and standards.
* Audits will be performed on a quarterly basis to verify the accuracy of records and compliance with regulations.

### 3.2. Penalties and Corrective Actions

* Non-compliance with established policies, SOPs, or standards may result in disciplinary action, including but not limited to:
	+ Verbal warnings
	+ Written warnings
	+ Suspension or termination of employment
* Corrective actions will be taken to address identified non-compliances.

### 3.3. Operational Governance

* The architecture team shall establish and maintain a governance framework for the design and development process.
* This framework will outline roles, responsibilities, and decision-making authorities for architects, engineers, and other stakeholders.
* The architecture team is responsible for ensuring that all projects adhere to this governance framework.

By following the guidelines outlined in this document, the Architecture department aims to deliver high-quality, scalable, and maintainable solutions that meet the needs of [Company Name] while ensuring compliance with relevant regulations and standards.
