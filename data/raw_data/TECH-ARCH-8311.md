### Document Scope and Executive Summary

This reference document outlines the core policies, standard operating procedures (SOPs), and governance framework for the Architecture department. The purpose of this document is to provide a comprehensive guide for architects, stakeholders, and relevant teams to ensure compliance with established standards, policies, and best practices.

The Architecture department's primary objective is to design and develop innovative solutions that meet business requirements while adhering to industry standards, security protocols, and organizational guidelines.

### Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

#### 1.1. Architecture Design Principles

* The department will adhere to the following principles when designing architectures:
	+ Scalability: Designs should be scalable to accommodate future growth.
	+ Security: Implement robust security measures to protect company assets.
	+ Interoperability: Ensure seamless integration with existing systems and technologies.
	+ Maintainability: Prioritize maintainable designs for easy updates and changes.

#### 1.2. Architecture Development Life Cycle

* The department will follow the below life cycle for architecture development:
	1. Requirements gathering
	2. Conceptual design
	3. Detailed design
	4. Implementation
	5. Testing and quality assurance
	6. Deployment
	7. Maintenance and continuous improvement

#### 1.3. Code Quality and Best Practices

* The department will adhere to the following best practices for coding:
	+ Adhere to established coding standards (e.g., naming conventions, indentation)
	+ Use version control systems (e.g., Git) for code management
	+ Implement automated testing and continuous integration/continuous deployment (CI/CD) pipelines
	+ Follow secure coding guidelines (e.g., input validation, error handling)

### Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

#### 2.1. Compliance Monitoring

* The Architecture department will be responsible for monitoring compliance with established policies, SOPs, and best practices.
* Regular audits and reviews will be conducted to ensure adherence to these guidelines.

#### 2.2. Penalties and Corrective Actions

* Failure to comply with established policies, SOPs, or best practices may result in:
	+ Verbal warnings
	+ Written warnings
	+ Suspension of privileges (e.g., access to certain systems or data)
	+ Termination of employment or contract

#### 2.3. Operational Governance

* The Architecture department will be responsible for the following operational governance:
	+ Establish and maintain documentation for all architectures, including design decisions, risks, and assumptions.
	+ Conduct regular review and update of architectures to ensure they remain relevant and effective.
	+ Develop and maintain a knowledge management system to capture lessons learned, best practices, and expertise.
