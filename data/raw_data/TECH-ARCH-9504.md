# ARCHITECTURE REFERENCE DOCUMENT: TECH-ARCH-9504
## Department: Architecture
## Author: Jeffery Cantu
## Effective Date: 2024-04-11

### Document Scope and Executive Summary

This document outlines the critical policies, procedures, and governance framework for the Architecture department. The purpose of this reference document is to provide a comprehensive guide for employees, stakeholders, and management on the expected behaviors, best practices, and operational guidelines for architectural design, development, and maintenance.

The scope of this document covers all aspects of architecture-related activities within the organization, including:

* Design principles and standards
* Development methodologies and tools
* Change management and version control
* Quality assurance and testing procedures
* Collaboration and communication protocols

### 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

#### 2.1 Design Principles and Standards

The Architecture department shall adhere to the following design principles and standards:

* Compliance with industry-standard architecture frameworks (e.g., microservices, monolithic)
* Adherence to established coding standards and best practices
* Use of approved technologies and tools for development and testing
* Integration with existing systems and services

#### 2.2 Development Methodologies and Tools

The Architecture department shall employ the following development methodologies and tools:

* Agile development framework (Scrum, Kanban)
* Version control system (Git, SVN)
* Integrated Development Environment (IDE) or code editor
* Collaboration tools (JIRA, Asana, Trello)

#### 2.3 Change Management and Version Control

The Architecture department shall follow the following change management and version control procedures:

* Formal change request process for architectural changes
* Use of approved version control system for tracking changes
* Regular backups and archiving of code repositories
* Automated testing and quality assurance processes

### 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

#### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

The Architecture department shall implement the following compliance monitoring procedures:

* Regular audits and reviews of architectural designs and implementations
* Compliance with organizational policies and standards
* Adherence to industry-standard best practices and guidelines

#### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

Failure to comply with the policies, procedures, and governance framework outlined in this document may result in the following penalties:

* Verbal warnings or written reprimands
* Suspension of architectural design authority
* Referral to HR or organizational disciplinary process

#### 3.3 Operational Governance

The Architecture department shall be governed by the following operational principles:

* Clear roles and responsibilities for architects, developers, and stakeholders
* Regular communication and collaboration among team members and stakeholders
* Continuous improvement and knowledge sharing through training, mentorship, and peer review
