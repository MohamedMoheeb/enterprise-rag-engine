# TECH-ARCH-2990: Architecture Reference Document
## Department: Architecture
## Author: Samantha Gillespie
## Effective Date: 2023-02-11


# 1. Document Scope and Executive Summary

This Architecture Reference Document (ARD) provides a comprehensive overview of the policies, procedures, and guidelines governing the Architecture department's activities within the organization. The ARD serves as a single source of truth for all architectural-related matters, ensuring consistency across projects, teams, and stakeholders.

The document covers the following key areas:

* Core policies and standard operating procedures
* Compliance monitoring, penalties, and operational governance

This document is intended for Architecture team members, project managers, product owners, and other stakeholders involved in architecture-related activities. It provides guidance on best practices, standards, and requirements for designing, developing, and maintaining the organization's software systems.

# 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

## Policy 1: Architecture Principles

The Architecture department shall adhere to the following principles:

* Scalability: Designs shall be scalable to accommodate future growth.
* Security: Designs shall prioritize security and minimize potential vulnerabilities.
* Interoperability: Designs shall ensure seamless integration with other systems and technologies.
* Flexibility: Designs shall remain flexible to accommodate changing business requirements.

## Procedure 1: Architecture Review Process

The architecture review process consists of the following stages:

1. **Initial Review**: The Architecture Lead reviews the design documents and provides feedback on adherence to architecture principles, standards, and guidelines.
2. **Iteration**: The architect revises the design based on the feedback received.
3. **Final Review**: The Architecture Lead conducts a final review to ensure compliance with architecture principles, standards, and guidelines.

## Procedure 2: Change Management

The change management process involves the following steps:

1. **Request for Change**: A request for change is submitted through the designated channels.
2. **Assessment**: The Architecture Lead assesses the impact of the proposed change on existing systems and architectures.
3. **Design**: The architect develops a revised design incorporating the requested changes.
4. **Review**: The architecture review process is followed to ensure compliance with architecture principles, standards, and guidelines.

# 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

## Compliance Monitoring

The Architecture department shall monitor compliance with this document through regular audits, reviews, and assessments.

## Penalties for Non-Compliance

 Failure to comply with the policies and procedures outlined in this document may result in:

* Verbal warnings
* Written warnings
* Suspension of architecture-related activities
* Termination of employment or contract

## Operational Governance

The Architecture department shall operate under the following governance structure:

* **Architecture Lead**: Responsible for overseeing the architecture review process, ensuring compliance with architecture principles, standards, and guidelines.
* **Architects**: Responsible for designing, developing, and maintaining software systems in accordance with this document.
* **Project Managers**: Responsible for managing projects and ensuring that architectural decisions align with project goals and objectives.

By following the policies, procedures, and guidelines outlined in this document, the Architecture department will ensure the development of high-quality software systems that meet business requirements and comply with organizational standards.
