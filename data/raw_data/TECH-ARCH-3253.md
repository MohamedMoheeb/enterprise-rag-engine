## 1. Document Scope and Executive Summary
This Architecture Reference Document (ARD) outlines the core policies, standard operating procedures, and guidelines for the Architecture department at [Company Name]. The document provides a comprehensive framework for ensuring effective design, development, and deployment of architectural solutions that align with business objectives.

The ARD aims to establish clear expectations and responsibilities for architects, stakeholders, and relevant teams, thereby promoting collaboration, consistency, and efficiency. This document is applicable to all employees involved in the architecture process, including architects, developers, quality assurance testers, and project managers.

## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures
### 2.1 Architecture Principles

The Architecture department shall adhere to the following principles:

* **Separation of Concerns**: Components are designed to perform specific functions without unnecessary dependencies.
* **Loose Coupling**: Modules are loosely coupled to facilitate independent development, testing, and maintenance.
* **High Cohesion**: Components focus on a single responsibility to promote modularity and scalability.

### 2.2 Architecture Design and Development

The following procedures apply to architecture design and development:

1. **Requirements Gathering**: Architects shall gather and document business requirements through collaboration with stakeholders and subject matter experts (SMEs).
2. **Architecture Design**: Architects shall create detailed, high-level designs for systems, applications, or services, considering scalability, performance, security, and maintainability.
3. **Design Review**: Architects shall conduct regular design reviews to ensure alignment with architecture principles and standards.
4. **Code Development**: Developers shall implement architecture designs according to established coding standards.

### 2.3 Architecture Change Management

The following procedures apply to architecture changes:

1. **Change Request**: Any proposed architecture change must be documented in a change request, including justification and expected impact.
2. **Architecture Review Board (ARB)**: The ARB shall review and approve or reject architecture changes based on their alignment with architecture principles and standards.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance
### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

The Architecture department shall ensure compliance with this ARD by:

* Conducting regular audits to verify adherence to architecture design and development procedures.
* Providing training and awareness programs for architects and stakeholders on architecture principles, standards, and best practices.

### 3.2 Penalties

 Failure to comply with the ARD may result in penalties, including but not limited to:

* **Verbal Warning**: A verbal warning shall be issued for minor infractions or non-compliance.
* **Written Warning**: A written warning shall be issued for more severe violations or repeated non-compliance.

### 3.3 Operational Governance

The Architecture department shall maintain and update this ARD as necessary, ensuring that it remains relevant, effective, and aligned with company objectives.
