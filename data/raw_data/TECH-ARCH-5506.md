# TECH-ARCH-5506: Architecture Reference Document

## Department: Architecture
## Author: Melanie Blevins
## Effective Date: 2020-06-21

### Document Scope and Executive Summary

This reference document outlines the core policies, standard operating procedures (SOPs), and guidelines for the Architecture department. The purpose of this document is to provide a centralized source of information on architectural design principles, standards, and best practices. This document applies to all employees working within the Architecture department.

The Architecture department is responsible for designing, developing, and maintaining the company's technology infrastructure, including software applications, databases, networks, and systems architecture. The team works closely with cross-functional teams to ensure that technical solutions align with business objectives.

### 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

#### 2.1 Design Principles

* The Architecture department will adhere to a service-oriented architecture (SOA) approach, focusing on modular, scalable, and maintainable systems.
* All architectural designs will prioritize security, reliability, and performance.
* Designs will be based on industry-standard technologies and frameworks.

#### 2.2 Development Standards

* Code reviews will be conducted for all code changes to ensure adherence to coding standards and best practices.
* The Architecture department will use version control systems (VCS) for managing code repositories.
* Developers will adhere to a consistent naming convention, formatting, and commenting style.

#### 2.3 Change Management

* Changes to architectural designs or technical infrastructure will be submitted through the company's change management process.
* All changes will be reviewed, approved, and tested before deployment.

### 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

#### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

* The Architecture department will maintain compliance with relevant industry standards, regulations, and company policies.
* Regular audits and reviews will be conducted to ensure adherence to these standards.

#### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

* Failure to comply with architectural design principles or development standards may result in code rework or disciplinary action.
* Repeated non-compliance may lead to additional penalties, including performance improvement plans or termination.

#### 3.3 Operational Governance

* The Architecture department will maintain a centralized repository of technical documentation and knowledge base.
* The team will participate in regular training and professional development activities to stay up-to-date with industry trends and best practices.

### Edge Cases and Exceptions

* In exceptional circumstances, the Architecture department may deviate from established design principles or standards. Such deviations must be documented, approved by management, and justified through a risk assessment.
* The team will work closely with stakeholders to identify and mitigate any potential risks or issues related to architectural changes or technical infrastructure updates.
