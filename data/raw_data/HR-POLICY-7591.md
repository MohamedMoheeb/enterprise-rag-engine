## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures
### 2.1 Recruitment and Hiring Practices

* All job postings must be approved by the hiring manager and HR prior to publication.
* Resumes will be reviewed and evaluated based on the job requirements and qualifications.
* Interviews will be conducted in person or via video conferencing, as needed.
* Job offers will be extended only after a thorough background check has been completed.

### 2.2 Employee Onboarding and Orientation

* All new hires must complete an online orientation program within their first week of employment.
* HR will provide a comprehensive onboarding package, including benefits information, company policies, and job expectations.
* Supervisors are responsible for conducting regular check-ins with new employees to ensure a smooth transition.

### 2.3 Performance Management and Evaluations

* Annual performance evaluations will be conducted by supervisors in collaboration with HR.
* Employees will receive feedback on their strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement.
* Development plans will be created to address employee growth and career advancement.

### 2.4 Compensation and Benefits Administration

* All compensation and benefits changes must be approved by the compensation committee and HR prior to implementation.
* Salary increases will be granted based on performance evaluations and market conditions.
* Employee benefits will be reviewed annually, with changes implemented as necessary.

### 2.5 Leave and Absence Policies

* Employees are entitled to [X] paid time off (PTO) per year, including vacation, sick leave, and family leave.
* PTO requests must be submitted at least [Y] days in advance.
* Bereavement leave will be granted for up to [Z] days, with additional documentation required.

### 2.6 Workplace Diversity and Inclusion Initiatives

* The company is committed to promoting diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI) through various initiatives, including training programs, employee resource groups, and community outreach.
* All employees are expected to participate in DEI efforts and promote a culture of inclusivity.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance
### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

* The HR department will monitor compliance with company policies and procedures through regular audits and employee feedback.
* Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

* Failure to comply with company policies and procedures may result in:
	+ Verbal warning
	+ Written warning
	+ Suspension
	+ Termination

### 3.3 Operational Governance

* The HR department will maintain accurate records of employee information, including personnel files, performance evaluations, and leave requests.
* All HR-related matters must be documented and approved by the HR manager or designee.
