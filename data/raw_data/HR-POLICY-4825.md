## 1. Document Scope and Executive Summary

This HR reference document outlines the core policies, standard operating procedures, and compliance requirements for the Human Resources department at [Company Name]. The purpose of this document is to provide a centralized resource for HR staff, management, and employees to ensure consistency and fairness in all aspects of employment.

The scope of this document includes:

* Employee relations and communications
* Recruitment and hiring processes
* Performance management and evaluation
* Compensation and benefits administration
* Leave and attendance policies
* Conflict resolution and disciplinary actions

## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Employee Classification and Job Descriptions

All employees will be classified as either exempt or non-exempt based on the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) guidelines. Job descriptions will be maintained and updated regularly to ensure accuracy and fairness in employee roles and responsibilities.

### 2.2 Recruitment and Hiring Process

The recruitment process will consist of the following steps:

1. Job posting and advertisement
2. Application review and shortlisting
3. Interview process with HR representatives and hiring managers
4. Reference checks and background verification (as necessary)
5. Offer extension and onboarding process

### 2.3 Performance Management and Evaluation

The performance management cycle will consist of the following steps:

1. Goal setting and objective establishment
2. Regular check-ins and feedback sessions
3. Mid-year review and evaluation
4. Annual review and evaluation

### 2.4 Leave and Attendance Policies

* Paid time off (PTO) will be granted to all employees based on company policies and federal/state laws.
* Sick leave will be provided according to company policy and state/federal regulations.
* Family and medical leave will be granted in accordance with the FMLA.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

HR will conduct regular compliance audits and reviews to ensure adherence to company policies, laws, and regulations.

### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

Any employee found to be in non-compliance with company policies or laws may face disciplinary actions up to and including termination of employment.

### 3.3 Operational Governance

HR will maintain accurate and up-to-date records of all HR-related activities, including but not limited to:

* Employee files
* Recruitment and hiring records
* Leave and attendance records
* Performance evaluations and reviews
