## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Architecture Principles

The following architecture principles shall guide all architecture-related activities:

* Scalability: Designs shall be scalable to accommodate growing demands
* Security: Designs shall prioritize security and confidentiality
* Interoperability: Designs shall ensure seamless integration with other systems
* Maintainability: Designs shall facilitate easy maintenance and updates

### 2.2 Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

The following SOPs shall govern architecture-related activities:

* Project Initiation: All projects shall follow the standard project initiation process, including project scope definition, timeline estimation, and resource allocation.
* Design Reviews: Regular design reviews shall be conducted to ensure compliance with architecture principles and standards.
* Code Quality: All code shall adhere to the company's coding standards and best practices.

### 2.3 Change Management

All changes to architecture designs, documentation, or processes shall be managed through a formal change management process, including:

* Request for Change (RFC) submission
* Review and approval by designated stakeholders
* Implementation and testing
* Verification and validation

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

The Architecture department shall maintain records of all architecture-related activities, including design documents, code repositories, and project status updates. Regular compliance monitoring shall be conducted to ensure adherence to architecture principles, standards, and SOPs.

### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

 Failure to comply with architecture principles, standards, or SOPs may result in:

* Warning or reprimand
* Additional training or education
* Loss of privileges or access
* Disciplinary action up to and including termination

### 3.3 Operational Governance

The Architecture department shall maintain a governance framework that includes:

* Roles and responsibilities: Clearly defined roles and responsibilities for architects, designers, and stakeholders.
* Communication channels: Established communication channels for collaboration, feedback, and issue resolution.
* Performance metrics: Regular monitoring of performance metrics, including project success rates, design quality, and customer satisfaction.

By following this reference document, the Architecture department can ensure consistency, quality, and efficiency in its architecture-related activities.
