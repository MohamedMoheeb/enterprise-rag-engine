## 2. Core Policies and Standard Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Architecture Principles

Our company's architectural principles are based on the following core values:

* **Modularity**: Design systems that are modular, scalable, and reusable.
* **Flexibility**: Develop architectures that can adapt to changing business needs and technology advancements.
* **Security**: Implement secure design practices to protect our customers' data and systems.
* **Innovation**: Encourage experimentation and innovation in architectural design.

### 2.2 SOPs for Architecture Design and Development

#### 2.2.1 System Architecture Review Process

The system architecture review process is a formal approval process that ensures the proposed architecture meets the company's standards and guidelines. The process includes:

* **Architecture Submission**: Architects submit their designs to the Architecture Review Committee (ARC) for review.
* **Peer Review**: ARC reviews the design and provides feedback to the architect.
* **Approval**: The design is either approved or rejected based on its compliance with company standards and guidelines.

#### 2.2.2 Code Quality Control

To ensure high-quality code, we have implemented the following SOPs:

* **Code Reviews**: All code changes are reviewed by peers or automated tools to identify potential issues.
* **Testing**: Thorough testing is performed to validate the functionality of the code.
* **Continuous Integration and Continuous Deployment (CI/CD)**: Our CI/CD pipeline ensures that all code changes are properly tested, validated, and deployed.

### 2.3 Compliance Monitoring

The Architecture department is responsible for monitoring compliance with company policies and SOPs. This includes:

* **Regular Audits**: Conduct regular audits to ensure compliance with architectural standards.
* **Incident Reporting**: Report any incidents or non-compliance issues to the appropriate stakeholders.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring, Penalties, and Operational Governance

### 3.1 Compliance Monitoring

The Architecture department will conduct regular monitoring activities to ensure compliance with company policies and SOPs. This includes:

* **Audits**: Conduct regular audits to identify potential compliance issues.
* **Risk Assessments**: Perform risk assessments to identify potential risks and take corrective actions.

### 3.2 Penalties for Non-Compliance

In the event of non-compliance, the following penalties will be imposed:

* **Verbal Warning**: A verbal warning will be issued to the architect or team lead.
* **Written Warning**: A written warning will be issued to the architect or team lead.
* **Suspension**: The architect or team lead may be suspended from their duties for a specified period.

### 3.3 Operational Governance

The Architecture department is responsible for making operational decisions that align with company policies and SOPs. This includes:

* **Change Management**: Implement change management processes to ensure that changes are properly assessed, approved, and implemented.
* **Decision-Making Processes**: Establish clear decision-making processes for the Architecture department.
